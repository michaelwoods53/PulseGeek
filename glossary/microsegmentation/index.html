Microsegmentation

Fine‑grained isolation of workloads or devices.

In detail

Microsegmentation limits lateral movement by enforcing detailed policy between entities. With Wi‑Fi 7, it can be implemented via roles, ACLs, and software‑defined overlays.

Synonyms

granular segmentation

Abbreviations

MICROSEG
