Programmable Power Supply

A USB‑C mode that fine‑tunes voltage and current.

In detail

Programmable Power Supply allows granular voltage steps for efficient fast charging. Some chargers support PPS in addition to PD, which can improve compatibility and thermal behavior.

Synonyms

USB‑C PPS, variable voltage

Abbreviations

PPS
