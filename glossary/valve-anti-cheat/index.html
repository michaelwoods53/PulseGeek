Valve Anti-Cheat

Valve’s automated system that detects and bans cheating on supported games.

In detail

Valve Anti-Cheat is a server-side and client-side system that detects known cheat signatures and behaviors. If cheating is confirmed, VAC issues permanent bans tied to the account. On Steam Deck, normal Proton use is allowed, but unapproved modifications can still trigger enforcement.
