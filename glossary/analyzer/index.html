Analyzer

Tool that decodes and inspects frames for troubleshooting and testing.

In detail

Analyzers capture and decode management, control, and data frames to validate standards compliance and diagnose issues such as MLO link setup or QoS marking in Wi‑Fi 7.

Synonyms

Protocol analyzer, Sniffer tool
