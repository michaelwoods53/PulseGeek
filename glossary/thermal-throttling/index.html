Thermal Throttling

Automatic slowing of a chip to reduce temperature.

In detail

Thermal throttling occurs when the APU reduces clock speeds to keep temperatures within safe limits. It protects hardware but can cause stutter and frame drops if cooling or settings are not optimized.

Synonyms

overheat throttling, temperature throttling

Abbreviations

TT
