EHT Puncturing

Skipping bad subchannels to keep wider channels usable.

In detail

Channel puncturing lets devices avoid interfered parts of a channel while still using the rest. This keeps wider Wi‑Fi 7 channels working in busy environments.

Synonyms

channel puncturing, EHT RU puncturing

Abbreviations

PUNCTURE
