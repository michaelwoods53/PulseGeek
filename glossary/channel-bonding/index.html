Channel Bonding

Combining adjacent channels to increase bandwidth.

In detail

Channel bonding aggregates two or more channels into a wider channel (e.g., 80+80 or 160 MHz). It boosts peak rates but can raise interference and reduce reuse.

Synonyms

wide channel aggregation

Abbreviations

CBOND

Related terms

  • Spectral Mask
  • Adjacent Channel Interference
