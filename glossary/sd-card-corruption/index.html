SD Card Corruption

When an SD card’s file system or data becomes unreadable.

In detail

Corruption can be caused by unsafe ejects, power loss, or bad formats. Onion OS often fails to boot or load assets until the card is repaired or reformatted.

Synonyms

corrupt sd card, damaged filesystem

Abbreviations

SDC
