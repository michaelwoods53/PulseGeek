MangoHUD

In-game overlay for FPS, frame time, and sensors on Linux.

In detail

MangoHUD is an overlay that shows FPS, frame times, and sensor data like temperatures. On the Steam Deck it helps visualize heat, power draw, and the effect of settings in real time.

Synonyms

mango hud overlay, linux hud

Abbreviations

MHUD
