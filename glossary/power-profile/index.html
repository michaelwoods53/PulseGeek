Power Profile

Saved settings that control performance and power use.

In detail

Power profiles bundle FPS caps, TDP, fan curves, and other toggles. Per‑game profiles on Steam Deck help keep gameplay smooth while maximizing battery life.

Synonyms

performance profile, battery profile

Abbreviations
