Interpreter

Executes guest instructions one by one.

In detail

An interpreter directly executes each instruction of the emulated CPU. It is accurate but much slower than JIT or dynarec approaches.

Synonyms

cpu interpreter

Related terms

  • Dynarec
  • JIT
