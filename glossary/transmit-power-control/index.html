Transmit Power Control

Adjusting transmit power to balance coverage and interference.

In detail

Transmit Power Control sets AP and client power to achieve target cell sizes and SNR while limiting overlap and interference, supporting stable coexistence in dense deployments.

Synonyms

power tuning, tpc

Abbreviations

TPC
