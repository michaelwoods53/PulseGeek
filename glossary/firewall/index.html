Firewall

Rules that control network traffic to and from a device.

In detail

The Onion OS firewall filters packets and NAT rules to protect services like SSH and FTP. It can limit ports, IP ranges, and zones using UCI or LuCI.

Synonyms

packet filter

Related terms

  • UCI
