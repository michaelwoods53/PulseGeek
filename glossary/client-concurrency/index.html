Client Concurrency

Number of active clients sharing airtime at once.

In detail

Client concurrency estimates how many devices transmit or receive simultaneously in a cell. It strongly affects airtime budgets and per‑user throughput.

Synonyms

simultaneous clients, concurrent users

Abbreviations

CCN

Related terms

  • Airtime Utilization
