Rollback

Returning to a previous version of a mod or setup.

In detail

Rolling back restores a working mod version or load order when a new update causes issues. Good versioning and backups make rollbacks safe and fast.

Synonyms

revert update

Related terms

  • Version Pinning
  • Semantic Versioning
