Cable Loss

Signal attenuation through RF cabling.

In detail

Cable loss increases with frequency and length, reducing power at the antenna. Using shorter runs and low-loss coax preserves link budget and improves SNR.

Synonyms

attenuation loss, coax loss

Abbreviations

CL
