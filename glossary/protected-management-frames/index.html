Protected Management Frames

Security that protects Wi‑Fi management frames from tampering.

In detail

PMF encrypts and authenticates management frames, reducing the risk of deauthentication attacks and other control-plane abuse. Modern enterprise deployments should require PMF for all clients.

Abbreviations
