Spatial Reuse

Reusing spectrum across nearby cells without harmful interference.

In detail

Spatial reuse allows overlapping networks to transmit at the same time when interference is acceptable. With puncturing, SR can further improve capacity by focusing on clean slices of spectrum.

Abbreviations
