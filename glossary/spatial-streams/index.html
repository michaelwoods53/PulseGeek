Spatial Streams

Independent data streams transmitted or received simultaneously.

In detail

Spatial streams use multiple antennas to send parallel data flows, increasing throughput. The number of supported streams depends on AP and client capabilities and link quality.

Synonyms

mimo streams

Abbreviations

SS
