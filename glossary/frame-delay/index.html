Frame Delay

Delays frame presentation to minimize latency.

In detail

Frame Delay holds back video output to reduce the time between input and display. On lower-power devices like Miyoo, conservative values avoid stutter while still trimming latency.

Synonyms

video frame delay

Abbreviations

FD

Related terms
