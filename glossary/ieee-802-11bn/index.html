IEEE 802.11bn

The IEEE working designation associated with WiFi 9.

In detail

IEEE 802.11bn is the working name for the WiFi 9 standard, defining PHY and MAC enhancements for efficiency, reliability, and power management. It targets dense deployments and heterogeneous device needs, including low-power IoT sensors.

Synonyms
