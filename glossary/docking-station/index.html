Docking Station

A device that expands ports like HDMI, DisplayPort, USB, and Ethernet for the Steam Deck.

In detail

A docking station connects to the Steam Deck’s USB-C port to add video outputs, power delivery, Ethernet, and USB peripherals. Some docks use HDMI, others use DisplayPort, and many provide charging while gaming.

Synonyms

USB-C dock, USB-C hub

Abbreviations

DOCK
