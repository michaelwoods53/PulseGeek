Ambient Temperature

Temperature of the surrounding environment.

In detail

Ambient temperature affects how well the Steam Deck can shed heat. Warmer rooms raise component temperatures and fan speeds, so small setting changes may have larger effects in hot environments.

Synonyms

room temperature, environment temperature

Abbreviations

Tamb

Related terms

  • Thermal Throttling
  • Fan Curve
  • Heat Soak
