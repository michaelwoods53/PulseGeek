Channel Utilization

A measure of how much the channel is busy over time.

In detail

Channel utilization reflects the share of time the channel is occupied. Before/after utilization charts help quantify the benefit of preamble puncturing when partial interference exists.

Synonyms

channel busy time, utilization rate

Abbreviations

CU
