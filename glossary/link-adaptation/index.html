Link Adaptation

Choosing data rates based on signal quality and interference.

In detail

Link adaptation selects MCS and spatial streams based on channel conditions. Puncturing changes the effective bandwidth and subcarrier quality, influencing the rate control algorithm.

Synonyms

rate adaptation

Abbreviations

LA

Related terms
