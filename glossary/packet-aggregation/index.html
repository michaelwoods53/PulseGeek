Packet Aggregation

Combining frames to reduce overhead per payload.

In detail

Aggregation techniques like A‑MPDU and A‑MSDU bundle multiple frames into a single transmission. This improves efficiency, lowers energy per bit, and shortens the time the radio must stay awake.

Synonyms

ampdu, amsdu

Abbreviations

AMPDU
