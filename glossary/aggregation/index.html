Aggregation

Combining multiple frames to reduce overhead and increase throughput.

In detail

Aggregation techniques like A‑MPDU and A‑MSDU reduce per-frame overhead. On 320 MHz channels, effective aggregation helps approach peak data rates while keeping latency manageable.

Synonyms

a-mpdu, a-msdu
