Bit Error Rate

The fraction of received bits that are in error.

In detail

BER is the ratio of erroneous bits to total transmitted bits over a period. It depends on SNR, modulation order, coding, and channel conditions and is fundamental for link budgeting and MCS selection.

Synonyms

error rate

Abbreviations

BER

Related terms

  • Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR)
  • Low-Density Parity-Check (LDPC)
