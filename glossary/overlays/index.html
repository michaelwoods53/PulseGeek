Overlays

Graphic frames drawn over the game screen for style or controls.

In detail

Overlays are static or interactive images placed above gameplay, often used for bezels or virtual buttons. On Miyoo, lightweight overlays maintain style without hurting performance.

Synonyms

bezels, screen overlays

Abbreviations
