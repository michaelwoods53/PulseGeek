Remote Play

Streaming games to the Steam Deck from another device.

In detail

Remote Play streams gameplay from a PC or cloud, shifting heavy rendering off the Steam Deck. This can significantly lower local heat and fan noise while maintaining playability.

Synonyms

steam remote play, streaming to deck

Abbreviations

RP
