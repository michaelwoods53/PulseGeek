Level of Detail

Swapping detail levels by distance to save performance.

In detail

Level of Detail uses multiple versions of an asset with different triangle counts. Engines switch models or materials based on screen size or distance to reduce cost without noticeable quality loss.

Synonyms

lod system

Abbreviations

LOD
