Undervolting

Reducing component voltage to lower power and heat.

In detail

Undervolting cuts voltage to decrease power and temperature. While common on PCs, it may not be officially supported on the Steam Deck; users should rely on safe software limits first.

Synonyms

voltage tuning, lowering voltage

Abbreviations

UV

  • PPT
  • Thermal Throttling
