Resource Unit (RU)

An OFDMA subchannel allocation to one or more clients.

In detail

A Resource Unit is a portion of bandwidth assigned to stations during OFDMA transmissions. With puncturing, RU planning must account for masked sub‑channels to keep utilization high and latency low.

Synonyms

ru allocation, ofdma subchannel

Abbreviations

RU
