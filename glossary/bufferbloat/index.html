Bufferbloat

Excessive queuing in network devices that adds latency under load.

In detail

Bufferbloat happens when routers or modems hold too much data in queues. Under upload or download load, gaming packets wait behind bulky transfers, spiking latency and jitter.

Synonyms

excess buffering
