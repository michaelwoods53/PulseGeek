CRT Shader

A shader that simulates CRT display characteristics.

In detail

CRT shaders emulate phosphor masks, scanlines, bloom, and curvature to recreate the look of classic displays. They can improve perceived authenticity at modern resolutions.

Synonyms

scanline shader, mask shader
