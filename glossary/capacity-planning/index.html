Capacity Planning

Estimating network resources needed for expected demand.

In detail

Capacity planning is the process of forecasting users, devices, and application loads, then translating that demand into access point counts, channel plans, and backhaul to meet performance targets and SLAs.

Synonyms

network capacity modeling, wireless sizing

Abbreviations

NCP

Related terms

  • Service Level Agreement
  • Airtime Utilization
  • Access Point Density
