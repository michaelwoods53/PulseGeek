Wi‑Fi Scanner

Software or hardware that lists networks and channel use.

In detail

A Wi‑Fi scanner detects SSIDs, signal levels, channel usage, and client details. It complements spectrum analysis by mapping neighbor networks for coexistence planning.

Synonyms

network scanner, ssid scanner

Abbreviations

SCANNER
