Autosave

Automatic saving of state or SRAM at intervals or exit.

In detail

Autosave can store save states or SRAM when exiting content or at set intervals. On Miyoo RetroArch, autosave reduces data loss risk when switching games quickly.

Synonyms

auto save

Abbreviations

AS
