Anti-Cheat

Systems and policies that detect and deter cheating in games.

In detail

Anti-cheat refers to technical controls and operational policies that prevent unfair advantages in games. It includes client and server validation, telemetry analysis, and enforcement actions to protect competitive integrity while minimizing harm to legitimate players.

Synonyms

cheat prevention, fair play protection

Abbreviations
