Mod Loader

Software that injects or loads mods into a game at runtime or startup.

In detail

A mod loader is a tool or framework that discovers and loads mods when a game starts or during runtime. It sets up environments, hooks, and sometimes dependency management to enable mod functionality.

Synonyms

loader
