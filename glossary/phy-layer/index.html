PHY Layer

The physical signaling layer that transmits and receives radio frames.

In detail

The PHY defines modulation, coding, channelization, and RF parameters. Wi‑Fi 7 adds 320 MHz channels, 4096‑QAM, and improvements for better spectral efficiency.

Synonyms

Physical layer, RF layer

Abbreviations

PHY

Related terms

  • MAC Layer
  • 4096‑QAM
  • 320 MHz Channel
