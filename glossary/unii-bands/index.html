UNII Bands

Sub-bands within 5 and 6 GHz used for Wi‑Fi channels.

In detail

UNII-5 through UNII-8 define segments of the 6 GHz band. Each segment has specific rules and available channels that impact channel planning and power settings.

Synonyms

U-NII bands

Abbreviations

UNII
