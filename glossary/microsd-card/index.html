microSD card

A small removable flash storage card used to expand Steam Deck storage.

In detail

A microSD card is a tiny removable flash memory device that adds storage capacity for games and files. On Steam Deck, it slots into the side and can store Steam libraries, ROMs, and media, depending on the file system and format.

Synonyms

sd card, memory card

Abbreviations

MICROSD

Related terms

  • UHS-I
  • A2 rating
  • V30 rating
