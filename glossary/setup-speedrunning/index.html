Setup (Speedrunning)

A repeatable sequence or alignment used to perform a trick.

In detail

A setup provides consistent inputs, positions, or cues to execute a trick with higher reliability. Setups help stabilize formerly luck-based or frame-tight maneuvers.

Synonyms

lineup, visual cue
