USB Power Delivery

A fast charging standard over USB‑C.

In detail

USB Power Delivery negotiates voltage and current over USB‑C to deliver efficient charging. The Steam Deck uses PD for safe, faster charging with compatible chargers and power banks.

Synonyms

USB-C PD, power delivery

Abbreviations

PD
