Guard Band

Unused spectrum reserved to reduce interference.

In detail

A guard band is a small slice of spectrum left unused between channels or services to minimize interference due to imperfect filters or out-of-band emissions.

Synonyms

frequency guard, guard spacing

Abbreviations

GB

Related terms

  • Spectral Mask
  • Channel Bonding
  • Adjacent-Channel Interference
