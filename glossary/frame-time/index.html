Frame Time

The time it takes to render one frame.

In detail

Frame time is the duration to render and present a single frame. Consistent frame times reduce stutter and make gameplay feel smooth even if FPS is not very high.

Synonyms

frametime, frame pacing

Related terms

  • Frames Per Second
  • Stuttering
  • VSync
