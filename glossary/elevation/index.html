Elevation

The vertical pointing angle of an antenna.

In detail

Elevation defines the vertical aim of the antenna’s beam. Adjusting down-tilt or up-tilt balances near-field and far-field coverage in multi-floor or high-ceiling spaces.

Synonyms

vertical angle, tilt

Abbreviations

EL
