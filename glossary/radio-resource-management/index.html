Radio Resource Management

Automated control of channels, power, and other RF settings.

In detail

Radio Resource Management uses algorithms to assign channels, set power, and adjust parameters over time, aiming to improve performance and reduce interference across a wireless network.

Synonyms

rrm, auto rf

Abbreviations

RRM

Related terms

  • Transmit Power Control
  • Dynamic Channel Assignment
  • OBSS Power Detection
