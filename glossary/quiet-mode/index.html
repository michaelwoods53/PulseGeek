Quiet Mode

Settings tuned to minimize fan noise and heat.

In detail

Quiet Mode refers to a profile that favors low wattage, capped FPS, and gentle fan curves. It is ideal for late-night sessions where comfort and silence matter most.

Synonyms

silent profile, low noise mode

Abbreviations

QM
