LC3

The Bluetooth LE Audio codec designed for efficiency and quality.

In detail

LC3 is part of Bluetooth LE Audio and aims to provide better quality at lower bitrates than SBC. When fully supported, it can improve efficiency and latency for Steam Deck accessories.

Synonyms

Low Complexity Communications Codec

Abbreviations
