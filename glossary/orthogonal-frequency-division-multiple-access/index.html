Orthogonal Frequency-Division Multiple Access

A scheme where multiple users share subcarriers simultaneously.

In detail

OFDMA allocates subsets of OFDM subcarriers to different users at the same time, improving efficiency and lowering latency in uplink and downlink scheduling.

Synonyms

multi-user ofdm

Abbreviations

OFDMA

Related terms

  • Orthogonal Frequency-Division Multiplexing
  • Resource Unit (RU)
