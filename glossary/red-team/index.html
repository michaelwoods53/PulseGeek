Red Team

Team emulating attackers to test defenses and response.

In detail

A red team conducts realistic attack scenarios against wireless networks, including evil twin, credential theft attempts, and segmentation bypass. Exercises validate detection, triage, and incident response.

Synonyms

adversary simulation

Abbreviations

RT
