Dual Polarization

An antenna supporting two orthogonal polarizations.

In detail

Dual polarization antennas transmit and receive two orthogonal polarizations simultaneously. This improves throughput and robustness for MIMO and high-density WiFi designs.

Synonyms

x-pol, cross-polarized

Abbreviations

DP
