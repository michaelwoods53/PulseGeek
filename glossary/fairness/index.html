Fairness

Ensuring outcomes are balanced and not systematically biased.

In detail

Fairness checks verify that loot, spawns, and difficulty do not favor or punish players unfairly. Designers use statistics, confidence intervals, and safeguards to keep outcomes in bounds.

Synonyms

anti-bias
