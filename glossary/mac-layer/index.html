MAC Layer

Sublayer controlling channel access, framing, and coordination.

In detail

The MAC layer manages channel access, frame exchange, and coordination functions like scheduling and QoS. Wi‑Fi 7 refines MAC efficiency for higher throughput and lower latency.

Synonyms

Medium Access Control, MAC sublayer

Abbreviations

MAC
