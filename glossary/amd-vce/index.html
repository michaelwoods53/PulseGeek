VCE

AMD’s hardware video encoder for real-time applications.

In detail

AMD VCE is a hardware encoder that accelerates video compression on AMD GPUs, allowing smooth game streaming with codecs like H.264 and HEVC for clients such as the Steam Deck.

Synonyms

AMD Video Coding Engine

Abbreviations

VCE

Related terms

  • NVENC
  • HEVC
  • AV1
