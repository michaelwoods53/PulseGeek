Steam ROM Manager

A tool that generates Steam shortcuts for ROMs and emulators.

In detail

Steam ROM Manager scans your game library and creates neatly organized Steam entries with artwork. It’s commonly used to add DuckStation games to Steam on desktop and Steam Deck.

Synonyms

srm, rom shortcuts
