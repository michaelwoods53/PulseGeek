A2DP

Bluetooth profile for high-quality stereo audio output.

In detail

A2DP is the Bluetooth profile that streams stereo audio from the Steam Deck to headphones or speakers. It uses codecs like SBC, AAC, or aptX and does not provide a high-quality microphone channel.

Synonyms

Advanced Audio Distribution Profile

Abbreviations

A2DP

Related terms

  • HFP
  • SBC
