Management Frames

Control messages that manage Wi‑Fi connections.

In detail

Management frames handle association, authentication, and control operations. In Wi‑Fi 7, PMF and BIP help secure these frames against spoofing and teardown attacks.

Synonyms

802.11 management

Abbreviations

MGMT

Related terms

  • PMF
  • BIP
  • WIPS
