I/O Bottleneck

A slowdown caused by limited read/write speed.

In detail

An I/O bottleneck occurs when storage cannot keep up with demand, causing menu lag or stutter during saves. Choosing a quality SD card and reducing background writes help on Miyoo.

Synonyms

storage bottleneck

Abbreviations

IO
