Incumbent

An existing licensed user that must be protected from interference.

In detail

Incumbents are licensed services operating in a band, such as fixed links or radar, that share spectrum with unlicensed systems and require protection through coordination or avoidance.

Synonyms

primary user, protected service

Abbreviations

PU
