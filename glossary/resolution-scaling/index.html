Resolution Scaling

Adjusts render resolution to meet performance targets.

In detail

Resolution scaling lowers or raises the internal resolution based on load to hit target frame rates. In streaming, it balances sharpness against latency and bandwidth.

Synonyms

dynamic resolution

Abbreviations

DRS
