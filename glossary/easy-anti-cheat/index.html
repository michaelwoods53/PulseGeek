Easy Anti-Cheat

A popular anti-cheat service used by many multiplayer games.

In detail

Easy Anti-Cheat provides detection and prevention of cheating through client services and server validation. Some games enable EAC support for Proton and Steam Deck, while others do not, affecting whether online play is possible.
