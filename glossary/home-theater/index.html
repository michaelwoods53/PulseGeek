Home Theater

An audio-video setup with a TV or projector and multi-speaker audio.

In detail

Home theater setups often include an AV receiver, speakers, and a TV with eARC. The Steam Deck can integrate via a dock’s HDMI output for big-screen gaming.

Synonyms

surround sound system, HT setup

Abbreviations

HT
