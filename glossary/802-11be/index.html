802.11be

The IEEE amendment defining Wi‑Fi 7 features and specifications.

In detail

802.11be is an IEEE 802.11 amendment that adds Extremely High Throughput. It formalizes features including 320 MHz channelization, Multi‑Link Operation, higher-order modulation, enhanced OFDMA, Multi‑RU, HARQ, and improved MAC efficiency.

Synonyms

IEEE 802.11be, Wi‑Fi 7 standard

Abbreviations

80211BE

Related terms

  • Wi‑Fi 7
  • Extremely High Throughput
  • IEEE 802.11
