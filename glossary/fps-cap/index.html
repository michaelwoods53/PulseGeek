FPS Cap

A ceiling on frames per second to save power and heat.

In detail

An FPS cap limits the maximum frame rate, cutting unnecessary rendering work. On the Steam Deck, capping at 30 or pairing 40 FPS with 40 Hz reduces power draw, temperatures, and fan noise while keeping smoothness acceptable.

Synonyms

frame rate limit, fps limiter

Abbreviations

FPS

  • Frame Pacing
