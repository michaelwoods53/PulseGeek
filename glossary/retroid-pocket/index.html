Retroid Pocket

A portable Android-based gaming handheld by Retroid that runs emulators and Android games.

In detail

Retroid Pocket is a family of Android handhelds designed for retro and modern emulation. It uses Android OS, supports Bluetooth and Wi‑Fi, and runs emulator apps and frontends. Setup includes system updates, controller pairing, storage, and emulator configuration.
