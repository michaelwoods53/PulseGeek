Azimuth

The horizontal pointing angle of an antenna.

In detail

Azimuth describes the horizontal orientation of an antenna’s main lobe around the compass. Correct azimuth alignment shapes coverage and reduces interference.

Synonyms

horizontal angle

Abbreviations

AZ
