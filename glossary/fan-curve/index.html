Fan Curve

Mapping of temperature to fan speed (RPM).

In detail

A fan curve defines how aggressively the fan ramps as temperatures rise. Smoother curves reduce abrupt noise, while steeper curves keep components cooler at the expense of louder operation.

Synonyms

fan profile, rpm curve

Abbreviations

FC
