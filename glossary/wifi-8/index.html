Wi‑Fi 8

The upcoming generation of Wi‑Fi aimed at higher efficiency, reliability, and capacity.

In detail

Wi‑Fi 8 refers to the next generation of IEEE 802.11 technology expected to improve spectral efficiency, latency, and multi‑user performance over prior generations. It is discussed in the context of wide channels, dense deployments, and advanced scheduling features such as preamble puncturing.

Synonyms

next-gen wifi, future wifi standard

Abbreviations

WIFI8
