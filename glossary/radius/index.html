RADIUS

A protocol for authentication, authorization, and accounting.

In detail

RADIUS centralizes authentication and policy enforcement for Wi‑Fi networks. With EAP‑TLS, it validates certificates and applies VLANs or ACLs to segment IoT devices securely.

Synonyms

AAA server

Abbreviations

RADIUS

Related terms

  • EAP‑TLS
  • WPA3 Enterprise
  • Network Segmentation
