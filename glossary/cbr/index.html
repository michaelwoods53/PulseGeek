CBR

Encoding mode with fixed bitrate.

In detail

Constant bitrate keeps the stream at a fixed rate to simplify delivery. It can be stable for latency but may waste bits on easy scenes and struggle on complex ones.

Synonyms

constant bitrate

Abbreviations

CBR
