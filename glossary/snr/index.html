SNR

A measure of signal strength relative to noise.

In detail

Signal-to-noise ratio shapes the usable MCS and error rates for each client. Accurate SNR inputs help the scheduler choose appropriate RU sizes and coding for robust OFDMA performance.

Synonyms

signal-to-noise ratio

Abbreviations
