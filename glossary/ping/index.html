Ping

A measure of round-trip network delay reported in milliseconds.

In detail

Ping reflects how quickly your device can send a message to a server and receive a reply. It is the most common indicator of gaming responsiveness and is affected by routing, congestion, and distance.

Synonyms

ping time, round-trip time

Abbreviations

RTT

Related terms

  • Network Latency
  • Traceroute
  • Jitter
