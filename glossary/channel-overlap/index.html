Channel Overlap

When nearby Wi‑Fi channels spill into each other and cause interference.

In detail

Overlap is common on 2.4 GHz, where only channels 1, 6, and 11 are non‑overlapping in many regions. Avoiding overlap reduces retries and packet loss for Steam Deck traffic.

Synonyms

adjacent channel interference
