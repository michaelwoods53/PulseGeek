Hidden Node

A client that cannot hear others and causes collisions.

In detail

Hidden nodes are stations that cannot detect each other and transmit simultaneously, creating collisions and reducing airtime efficiency, especially in dense deployments.

Synonyms

hidden terminal, hidden station

Abbreviations

HN
