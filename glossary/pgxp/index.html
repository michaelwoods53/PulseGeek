PGXP

A technique that reduces PS1 texture warping and wobble.

In detail

PGXP improves geometric precision beyond original PS1 hardware, reducing vertex jitter and texture wobble. DuckStation offers PGXP options that can enhance visuals but may affect compatibility in rare cases.

Synonyms

precision geometry transformations
