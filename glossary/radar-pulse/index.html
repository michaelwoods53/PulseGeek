Radar Pulse

Detected radar signal that triggers DFS behavior.

In detail

A radar pulse detected on DFS channels requires devices to react per regulations. Designers must ensure puncturing and channel changes maintain compliance and service continuity.

Synonyms

Abbreviations

RADAR
