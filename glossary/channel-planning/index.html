Channel Planning

Selecting channels and widths to manage coverage and capacity.

In detail

Channel planning sets the foundation for performance in dense or noisy areas. With puncturing, planners can maintain wide channels while masking interfered slices.

Synonyms

rf planning, channel design

Abbreviations

CP
