Joystick Drift

Unwanted movement input when the joystick is not being touched.

In detail

Drift can be caused by wear, dust, or sensor issues. Software calibration and dead zone adjustments help, but severe drift may require hardware service.

Synonyms

stick drift

Abbreviations

DRIFT
