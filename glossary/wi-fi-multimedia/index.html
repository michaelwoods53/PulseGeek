Wi‑Fi Multimedia

A Wi‑Fi feature that prioritizes voice, video, and best-effort traffic classes.

In detail

Wi‑Fi Multimedia is a wireless QoS mechanism that maps traffic into access categories like voice and video. Enabling WMM helps ensure time-sensitive packets from the Steam Deck receive better airtime scheduling on the Wi‑Fi network.

Synonyms

WMM, wireless multimedia extensions

Abbreviations

WMM
