Noise Floor

The baseline RF noise level in an environment.

In detail

The noise floor is the aggregate background RF energy measured in an area. Monitoring changes helps detect new interference sources and validate coexistence plans.

Synonyms

rf noise, background noise

Abbreviations

NF

Related terms

  • Signal-to-Noise Ratio
