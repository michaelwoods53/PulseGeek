Protocol Analyzer

Software or hardware that decodes Wi‑Fi frames for analysis.

In detail

A protocol analyzer captures and decodes frames to reveal capabilities, triggers, and scheduler behavior. With puncturing, it confirms mask signaling and client compatibility.

Synonyms

packet analyzer, frame analyzer

Abbreviations

PA

Related terms

  • Packet Capture
  • Wireshark
  • Resource Unit (RU)
