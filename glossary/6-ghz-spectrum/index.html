6 GHz Spectrum

New Wi-Fi bands that add wide, cleaner channels.

In detail

The 6 GHz spectrum provides many contiguous channels supporting 160 and 320 MHz widths, reducing interference and enabling higher throughput for EHT.

Synonyms
