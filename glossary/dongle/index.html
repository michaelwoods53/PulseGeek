Dongle

A small device that adds wireless or audio features via USB.

In detail

Bluetooth or 2.4 GHz dongles can add low-latency audio paths to the Steam Deck. They may include codec support like aptX LL or Adaptive for better gaming response.

Synonyms

USB adapter, transmitter

Related terms

  • aptX LL
  • 2.4 GHz wireless
