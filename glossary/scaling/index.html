Scaling

Adjusting how pixels are mapped to the display’s resolution.

In detail

Scaling controls how a rendered image fits a screen. Integer scaling preserves sharpness for pixel art, while other methods stretch to fill the screen. Steam Deck offers scaling controls per game and system-wide.

Synonyms

image scaling, integer scaling

Abbreviations

SCL

Related terms

  • Resolution
  • FSR
  • Aspect Ratio
