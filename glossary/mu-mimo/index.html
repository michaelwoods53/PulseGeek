MU‑MIMO

A technique for sending data to multiple users at once using spatial streams.

In detail

MU‑MIMO leverages multiple antennas and beamforming to serve several clients simultaneously. With punctured channels, MU‑MIMO benefits depend on available clean subcarriers and the scheduler’s pairing of users.

Synonyms

multi-user mimo

Abbreviations

MU-MIMO
