Passpoint

A standard for secure, seamless Wi‑Fi onboarding and roaming.

In detail

Passpoint enables devices to automatically discover and connect to trusted Wi‑Fi networks using credentials like SIM, certificates, or accounts. It improves user experience for guest and enterprise roaming scenarios.
