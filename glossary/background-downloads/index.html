Background Downloads

Game or system downloads that continue while gaming.

In detail

Background downloads keep the network and storage active, increasing power draw. Disabling them on Steam Deck can reduce battery drain during play sessions.

Synonyms

auto updates, background updates

Abbreviations

BGDL
