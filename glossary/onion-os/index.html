Onion OS

A community firmware that enhances retro handhelds with an easy menu, updates, and tools.

In detail

Onion OS is a custom operating system for certain retro handhelds that adds a streamlined interface, curated apps, and organized folders for ROMs, BIOS, saves, and themes. It focuses on simple setup, safe updates, and better usability.
