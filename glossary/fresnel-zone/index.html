Fresnel Zone

The 3D area around the line-of-sight path that radio waves occupy.

In detail

Obstructions in the Fresnel zone can cause diffraction and reduce signal quality, especially for long corridors or outdoor links. Clear zones improve stability and throughput.

Synonyms

fresnel clearance

Abbreviations

FRESNEL
