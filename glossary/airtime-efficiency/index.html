Airtime Efficiency

How effectively a network uses available transmission time.

In detail

Airtime efficiency improves when overhead is minimized and traffic is well scheduled. Higher efficiency shortens client wake times and directly supports longer battery life.

Synonyms

airtime utilization, channel efficiency

Abbreviations

ATE

Related terms

  • OFDMA
  • Scheduler
  • Packet Aggregation
