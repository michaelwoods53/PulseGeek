Radar

High-power licensed signals that WiFi must avoid in DFS bands.

In detail

Radar signals appear as distinct pulses or sweeps that must be detected by DFS. When found, WiFi vacates the channel to prevent interference with incumbents.

Synonyms

incumbent detection

Abbreviations

RADAR
