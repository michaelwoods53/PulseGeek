Standard Power Access Point

A 6 GHz AP class that requires AFC approval for channels and power.

In detail

Standard power access points can transmit at higher levels on 6 GHz but must query an AFC system to receive allowed channels and EIRP. These are used for wider coverage, including certain outdoor scenarios.
