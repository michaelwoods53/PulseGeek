Band Steering

Router feature that nudges clients to 5 GHz instead of 2.4 GHz.

In detail

Band steering helps dual-band clients choose the less congested band, often 5 GHz, to improve throughput. Proper tuning can keep the Steam Deck on the faster band without frequent disconnects.

Synonyms

smart connect, band selection

Abbreviations

NA
