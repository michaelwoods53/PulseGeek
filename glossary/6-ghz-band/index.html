6 GHz band

A Wi‑Fi band offering wide channels and lower interference.

In detail

The 6 GHz band provides additional spectrum for Wi‑Fi with many clean channels and support for wide channel widths. It reduces congestion and enables high throughput when client and AP support are present, subject to local regulations.
