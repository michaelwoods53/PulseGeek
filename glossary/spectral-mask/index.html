Spectral Mask

Regulatory and standard limits on transmitted power over frequency.

In detail

The spectral mask defines permissible transmit power versus frequency offset. It ensures transmissions fit within assigned channels and limit out‑of‑band emissions, which is essential when evaluating the effects of puncturing and analyzer measurements.

Synonyms

spectrum mask, emission mask

Abbreviations

SM
