Certificate Lifecycle Management

Processes to issue, renew, and revoke digital certificates.

In detail

Certificate lifecycle management automates enrollment, rotation, and revocation for user and device certificates used in EAP‑TLS. It prevents outages and reduces security gaps from expired credentials.

Synonyms

cert management

Abbreviations

CLM

Related terms

  • Certificate Authority
  • EAP-TLS
