DXVK

A translation layer that maps DirectX 9/10/11 to Vulkan.

In detail

DXVK converts DirectX calls to Vulkan for performance and compatibility on Linux. While mostly graphics-related, certain DXVK versions can indirectly impact stability in online games by affecting overall runtime behavior.

Synonyms

DirectX to Vulkan

Abbreviations

DXVK
