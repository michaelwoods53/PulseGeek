Decay

A timed loss of value, power, or ownership.

In detail

Decay removes value from items or currencies over time through upkeep fees or expiration. It helps regulate hoarding and stabilizes markets.

Synonyms

upkeep

Related terms

  • Durability
  • Currency Sink
