HDMI

A video and audio interface used to connect the Steam Deck to TVs and monitors.

In detail

HDMI carries digital video and audio. With a dock or adapter, the Steam Deck can output HDMI to TVs and monitors at various resolutions and refresh rates, depending on cable and device support.

Synonyms

High-Definition Multimedia Interface
