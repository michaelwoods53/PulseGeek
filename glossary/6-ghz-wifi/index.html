6 GHz Wi‑Fi

Wi‑Fi operation in the 6 GHz band offering more spectrum.

In detail

6 GHz provides many clean channels for wide channel bonding and lower interference. It requires compatible clients and careful placement due to higher attenuation through walls.

Synonyms

wifi 6e and above

Abbreviations

6GHZ
