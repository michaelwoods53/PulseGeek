Steam Remote Play

Valve’s system for streaming PC games to other devices over a network.

In detail

Steam Remote Play is Valve’s feature that lets you run a game on a PC and stream video, audio, and inputs to another device like a Steam Deck over LAN or the internet. It compresses the game feed and relays controller or keyboard input back to the host PC.
