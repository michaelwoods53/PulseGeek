Refresh Rate

How often the screen updates per second.

In detail

Refresh rate, measured in hertz, determines how many times the screen updates each second. Lowering refresh rate to match an FPS cap, like 40 Hz with 40 FPS, reduces GPU load, heat, and noise on the Steam Deck.

Synonyms

display hz, screen refresh

Abbreviations

Hz

Related terms

  • FPS Cap
  • Frame Pacing
  • VSync
