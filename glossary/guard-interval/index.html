Guard Interval

A time gap that protects against multipath interference.

In detail

The guard interval, implemented as a cyclic prefix in OFDM, reduces intersymbol interference from multipath. Shorter GI increases efficiency but needs cleaner channels.

Synonyms

gi

Abbreviations

GI
