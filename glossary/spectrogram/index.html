Spectrogram

A plot showing signal power across frequency and time.

In detail

A spectrogram, often displayed as a waterfall plot, helps visualize when and where RF energy occurs. Patterns reveal hopping signals, continuous waves, and intermittent bursts.

Synonyms

waterfall, heatmap plot

Abbreviations

SPEC
