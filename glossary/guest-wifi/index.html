Guest Wi‑Fi

Public or visitor wireless access separated from internal resources.

In detail

Guest Wi‑Fi provides internet access for visitors using isolated networks and policies. Strong designs use OWE or WPA3, captive portals with minimal data collection, and strict segmentation from corporate resources.

Synonyms

visitor Wi‑Fi
