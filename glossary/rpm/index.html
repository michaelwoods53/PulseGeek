RPM

Fan speed measured in revolutions per minute.

In detail

RPM shows how fast the fan spins. Monitoring RPM alongside temperature and wattage helps verify whether a fan curve or power change reduced noise as expected.

Synonyms

fan speed, revolutions per minute

Abbreviations

RPM
