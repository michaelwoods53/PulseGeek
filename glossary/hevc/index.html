HEVC

High‑Efficiency Video Coding, successor to H.264.

In detail

HEVC compresses better than H.264 at the same quality but may involve licensing. It is widely hardware‑accelerated and common in cloud and mobile streaming.

Synonyms

h.265

Abbreviations

HEVC
