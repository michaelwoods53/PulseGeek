NTSC

A television and game region standard used in Japan and the Americas.

In detail

NTSC is a video and regional standard. PS1 saves from NTSC versions of games may not load on PAL versions and vice versa without conversion.

Synonyms

ntsc-u, ntsc-j

Abbreviations

NTSC
