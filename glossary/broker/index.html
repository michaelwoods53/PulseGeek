Broker

A server that routes publish/subscribe messages.

In detail

An MQTT broker manages topics, sessions, and QoS for IoT messaging. Scalable brokers like EMQX and HiveMQ support large Wi‑Fi sensor fleets with clustering and persistence.

Synonyms

MQTT broker
