Ghosting

Smearing trails behind moving objects.

In detail

Ghosting appears when temporal methods like TAA or upscalers reuse old frame data incorrectly. Tuning sharpening, quality modes, or motion vectors can reduce it.

Synonyms

trailing artifacts

Related terms

  • Temporal Anti-Aliasing
  • Temporal Super Resolution
  • Sharpening
