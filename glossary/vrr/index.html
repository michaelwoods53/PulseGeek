VRR

A standard for adaptive refresh displays.

In detail

VRR enables displays to change refresh dynamically to match the content’s frame rate, reducing tearing and improving perceived smoothness.

Synonyms

variable refresh rate

Abbreviations

VRR

Related terms

  • Adaptive Sync
  • Frame Rate
