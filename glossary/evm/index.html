EVM

A measure of modulation quality that impacts achievable rates.

In detail

Error Vector Magnitude quantifies signal distortion relative to ideal constellation points. Elevated EVM on certain subcarriers may motivate puncturing to improve overall link reliability.

Synonyms

error vector magnitude

Abbreviations

EVM
