Frame Advance

A practice feature that advances gameplay one frame at a time.

In detail

Frame advance lets runners study and practice precise inputs by moving forward one frame per button press, typically in emulators or TAS tools. It is used only for practice, not live attempts.

Synonyms

frame stepping
