Deauthentication Attack

An attack that forces clients off Wi‑Fi by spoofing management frames.

In detail

Deauthentication attacks send forged management frames to disconnect clients. WPA3 with Protected Management Frames prevents spoofing and should be enforced to mitigate this threat.

Synonyms

deauth attack

Abbreviations

DEAUTH

Related terms

  • Protected Management Frames
  • WIDS/WIPS
