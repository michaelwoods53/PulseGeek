Cyclic Prefix

A copy of the symbol tail added to resist multipath.

In detail

The cyclic prefix, often called the guard interval, is prepended to each OFDM symbol to prevent intersymbol interference and maintain subcarrier orthogonality in multipath channels.

Synonyms

guard interval

Abbreviations

CP

Related terms

  • Orthogonal Frequency-Division Multiplexing
  • Doppler Shift
