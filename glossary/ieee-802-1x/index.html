IEEE 802.1X

Port-based network access control for authentication.

In detail

802.1X is a framework that authenticates devices before granting network access. In Wi‑Fi, it integrates with WPA3-Enterprise and EAP methods to enforce identity and policy on clients.

Synonyms

dot1x

Abbreviations

802.1X

Related terms

  • EAP-TLS
  • Network Access Control
  • WPA3-Enterprise
