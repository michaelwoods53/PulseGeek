Certificate Enrollment

Processes that provision device certificates at scale.

In detail

Certificate enrollment uses protocols like SCEP or EST to request, issue, and renew device certificates. It underpins EAP‑TLS onboarding and lifecycle management for IoT fleets.

Synonyms

SCEP, EST

Related terms

  • EAP‑TLS
  • RADIUS
  • Zero Touch Provisioning
