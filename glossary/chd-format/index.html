CHD

A compressed format for disc or HDD images used by emulators.

In detail

CHD compresses disc or hard drive images while preserving data integrity. It reduces storage needs and can include metadata for accurate emulation.

Synonyms

compressed hunks of data

Abbreviations

CHD
