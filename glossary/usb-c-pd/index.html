USB-C PD

USB Power Delivery standard for negotiating charge wattage.

In detail

USB-C Power Delivery negotiates voltage and current between charger and device. Correct PD wattage can stabilize power draw when gaming, which helps manage heat while charging.

Synonyms

power delivery, usb-c charging

Abbreviations

PD
