Antenna Sectorization

Dividing coverage into sectors using directional antennas.

In detail

Antenna sectorization uses directional panels or arrays to split a large area into smaller coverage zones. Stadiums benefit by focusing energy into seating sections, reducing interference and increasing reuse.

Synonyms

sector antennas

Abbreviations
