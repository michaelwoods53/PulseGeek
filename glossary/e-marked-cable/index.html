E‑Marked Cable

A USB‑C cable with a chip that reports its capabilities.

In detail

E‑marked cables advertise safe voltage and current limits for USB‑C PD. Using an e‑marked cable with Steam Deck ensures reliable charging and proper power negotiation.

Synonyms

usb‑c e‑marker, 100w cable

Abbreviations

EMC
