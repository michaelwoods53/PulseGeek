Overscan

A TV setting that cuts off edges or leaves borders on the picture.

In detail

Overscan and underscan change how the image fits the screen. On Steam Deck, adjusting TV picture size or GPU scaling often fixes cut-off UI or black borders.

Synonyms

underscan, picture size

Abbreviations

OSCAN
