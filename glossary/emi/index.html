Electromagnetic Interference

Unwanted electromagnetic energy from electrical devices.

In detail

EMI originates from sources like motors, power supplies, and lighting. It can radiate or conduct into WiFi bands, degrading performance and stability.

Synonyms

electrical noise

Abbreviations

EMI
