Internet Service Provider

A company that provides internet access to customers.

In detail

ISPs connect homes to the wider internet and choose routes, peering, and traffic policies. Their infrastructure and congestion levels affect gaming latency.

Synonyms

broadband provider

Abbreviations

ISP
