Target Wake Time (TWT)

A power‑saving feature that schedules client wake and sleep times.

In detail

Target Wake Time coordinates when clients wake to send or receive data. With puncturing, TWT plans should align with clean sub‑channel availability to avoid contention.

Synonyms

scheduled wake time

Abbreviations

TWT
