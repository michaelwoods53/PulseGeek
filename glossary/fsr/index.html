FSR

An upscaling technique that enhances resolution and sharpness.

In detail

FSR is a spatial upscaler that increases apparent resolution using image processing. Some emulators or overlays can apply it to improve clarity with modest GPU cost.

Synonyms

fidelityfx super resolution

Abbreviations
