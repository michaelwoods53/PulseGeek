RetroArch

A frontend for emulators that manages cores and ROMs.

In detail

RetroArch runs multiple emulator cores under one interface. On Steam Deck, its ROMs and BIOS files are often stored on the microSD card for space and portability.

Synonyms

libretro frontend

Abbreviations
