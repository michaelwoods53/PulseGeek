Aspect Ratio

The width-to-height proportion of a display, like 16:9 or 21:9.

In detail

Aspect ratio determines the shape of the image. On Steam Deck, ultrawide displays may require special configuration to avoid stretching or black bars.

Synonyms

screen ratio, ultrawide ratio

Abbreviations

AR
