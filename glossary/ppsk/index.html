Private Pre‑Shared Key

Unique PSK per user or device for improved isolation.

In detail

PPSK replaces a single shared passphrase with individual keys, supporting role‑based policies on Wi‑Fi 7 without full 802.1X for certain device types.

Synonyms

per‑user PSK

Abbreviations

PPSK

Related terms

  • WPA3
  • SSID
  • NAC
