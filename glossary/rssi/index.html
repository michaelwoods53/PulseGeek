RSSI

A measure of received signal power at the client or AP.

In detail

RSSI indicates signal power relative to device-defined scales. Designers use RSSI thresholds alongside SNR to ensure reliable MCS rates and roaming behavior.

Synonyms

received signal strength indicator

Abbreviations

RSSI
