RF Interference

Noise that disrupts wireless signals.

In detail

RF interference from other networks or devices increases retries and delay. Site surveys and proper channel plans reduce jitter and packet loss.

Synonyms

radio interference

Abbreviations

RFI

Related terms

  • BSS Coloring
  • SNR
