Identity Provider

Service that authenticates users and provides identity assertions.

In detail

An Identity Provider like Okta or Microsoft Entra ID authenticates users and can integrate with RADIUS and NAC to drive Wi‑Fi access policies based on roles and groups.

Synonyms

IdP

Abbreviations

IDP

Related terms

  • Network Access Control
  • RADIUS
