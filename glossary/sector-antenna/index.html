Sector Antenna

A directional antenna covering a defined arc, often 60°–120°.

In detail

Sector antennas concentrate energy over a specific azimuth with defined elevation. They are common in stadiums and outdoor campuses to scale capacity and control interference.

Synonyms

sector panel

Abbreviations
