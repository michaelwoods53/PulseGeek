Adjacent Channel Interference

Interference from signals in neighboring channels.

In detail

Adjacent Channel Interference occurs when energy leaks into nearby channels. Puncturing may omit the affected slice to protect performance while keeping most of a wide channel usable.

Synonyms

aci

Abbreviations

ACI

Related terms

  • Spectral Mask
  • Co‑Channel Interference
  • Channel Mask
