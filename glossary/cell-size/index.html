Cell Size

Effective coverage area of an AP on a band.

In detail

Cell size is the area served by an AP at a target minimum data rate. Designing smaller cells can improve capacity by enabling more spatial reuse and lower contention.

Synonyms

coverage radius, cell radius

Abbreviations

CSZ

Related terms

  • Access Point Density
  • Spatial Reuse
  • RSSI
