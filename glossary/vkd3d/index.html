VKD3D

A translation layer for DirectX 12 over Vulkan.

In detail

VKD3D translates DirectX 12 graphics commands into Vulkan calls, allowing many DX12 games to run on Linux via Proton, often with continued compatibility updates.

Synonyms

DirectX 12 to Vulkan

Abbreviations

VKD3D
