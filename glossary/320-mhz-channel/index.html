320 MHz Channel

The widest channelization option in Wi‑Fi 7, available primarily in 6 GHz.

In detail

A 320 MHz channel doubles the maximum channel width compared to Wi‑Fi 6E. It increases peak PHY rates but requires clean spectrum and careful planning to manage interference and coexistence.

Synonyms

320 MHz bandwidth, wide channel Wi‑Fi 7

Abbreviations

320MHZ

Related terms

  • 6 GHz
