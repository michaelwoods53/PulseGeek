Regulatory Domain

Region-specific RF rules for power, channels, and DFS.

In detail

A regulatory domain defines allowed channels, power limits, and DFS rules. Designs must comply to avoid illegal transmissions and unexpected interference.

Synonyms

country code

Abbreviations
