DFS

Radar detection rules that govern certain 5 GHz and 6 GHz channels.

In detail

Dynamic Frequency Selection requires Wi‑Fi devices to detect radar and alter operation to avoid interference. When puncturing is used, designers must ensure masks and behavior remain compliant with DFS.

Synonyms

dynamic frequency selection

Abbreviations

DFS

Related terms

  • Spectral Mask
  • Channel Mask
  • Radar Pulse
