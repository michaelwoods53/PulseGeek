Benchmark

A test that measures storage performance metrics.

In detail

Benchmarks report speeds like sequential reads and random IOPS. On Steam Deck, benchmarking a microSD helps validate card health and pick the best library location.

Synonyms

performance test

Abbreviations

BM

Related terms

  • Read-write speed
  • UHS-I
  • A2 rating
