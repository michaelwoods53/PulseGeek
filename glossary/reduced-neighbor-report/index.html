Reduced Neighbor Report

Information element that helps clients discover 6 GHz BSSs via lower bands.

In detail

The Reduced Neighbor Report advertises 6 GHz BSS information in 2.4/5 GHz beacons and probe responses, helping clients discover and roam efficiently into 6 GHz Wi‑Fi 7 networks.

Synonyms

RNR, Neighbor report 6 GHz

Abbreviations

RNR
