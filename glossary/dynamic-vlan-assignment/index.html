Dynamic VLAN Assignment

Assigning VLANs per user or device via RADIUS attributes.

In detail

Dynamic VLAN Assignment uses RADIUS to place clients into VLANs based on identity or posture, enabling scalable segmentation and simplified SSID design in enterprise Wi‑Fi.

Synonyms

per‑user VLAN, RADIUS VLAN

Abbreviations

DVA
