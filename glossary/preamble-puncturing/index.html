Preamble Puncturing

A method to use most of a wide channel while avoiding a narrow interfered portion.

In detail

Preamble puncturing allows an access point to reuse the clean parts of a wide channel by omitting an interfered sub‑channel during the preamble. This preserves effective bandwidth and reduces retries when a small slice of spectrum is busy or restricted.

Synonyms

punctured preamble, partial channel reuse

Abbreviations

PP

Related terms

  • Channel Mask
  • Spectral Mask
  • Effective Bandwidth
