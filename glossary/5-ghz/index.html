5 GHz

A faster Wi‑Fi band with shorter range and less interference.

In detail

The 5 GHz band offers higher throughput and lower latency, ideal for game streaming. It may struggle through walls and at longer distances.

Synonyms

5g band (wifi)

Abbreviations

5G-WIFI
