Wi‑Fi 9

A future Wi‑Fi generation focused on higher efficiency, reliability, and throughput.

In detail

Wi‑Fi 9 refers to the next generation of IEEE 802.11 technology aiming to improve spectral efficiency, reliability, and peak and sustained throughput. It builds on OFDM/OFDMA, advanced modulation, stronger coding, and smarter MIMO and link adaptation.

Synonyms

next-gen wi-fi, future wi-fi standard

Abbreviations

WIFI9

Related terms

  • OFDMA
  • Modulation and Coding Scheme (MCS)
