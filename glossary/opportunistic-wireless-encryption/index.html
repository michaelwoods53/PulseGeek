Opportunistic Wireless Encryption

Encryption for open networks without user credentials.

In detail

OWE provides individualized encryption on open Wi‑Fi networks, improving privacy by removing cleartext traffic. It is useful for guest access when full authentication is not required.

Synonyms

enhanced open

Abbreviations

OWE
