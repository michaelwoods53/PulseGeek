Link Budget

A calculation of gains and losses from transmitter to receiver.

In detail

A link budget sums transmit power, antenna gains, feeder losses, and path loss to estimate received signal levels. It guides antenna gain, cable length, and placement choices.

Synonyms

rf budget, path loss budget

Abbreviations

LB

Related terms

  • RSSI
  • SNR
  • Free Space Path Loss
