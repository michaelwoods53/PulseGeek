Active Site Survey

Field measurement of Wi‑Fi signal and capacity.

In detail

An active site survey collects live measurements of signal, noise, and throughput to validate design assumptions and SLA performance in production.

Synonyms

on site survey, walk test

Abbreviations

ASS
