TDP

The designed power draw a cooling system can handle.

In detail

Thermal Design Power is the target wattage that cooling must dissipate. Lowering effective TDP on the Steam Deck reduces heat and fan noise, sometimes at the cost of frame rate.

Synonyms

thermal design power, power limit

Abbreviations

TDP

Related terms

  • PPT
  • Thermal Throttling
  • Fan Curve
