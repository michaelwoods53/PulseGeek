UPnP

A protocol that auto-creates router port mappings.

In detail

UPnP can open required ports for peer-to-peer matchmaking without manual rules. If it fails, Steam Deck players may need explicit port forwarding to fix strict NAT issues.

Synonyms

universal plug and play

Abbreviations

UPnP
