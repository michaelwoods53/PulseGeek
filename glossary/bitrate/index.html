Bitrate

Amount of video data sent per second.

In detail

Bitrate controls video quality and latency trade‑offs. Higher bitrates improve detail but require more bandwidth and can increase latency if buffers fill during congestion.

Synonyms

stream rate

Abbreviations

kbps
