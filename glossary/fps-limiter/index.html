FPS Limiter

A cap on the maximum frames per second.

In detail

An FPS limiter locks the emulator to a target rate to stabilize pacing and prevent runaway frames. On Miyoo, it works with VSync to reduce stutter and input variance.

Synonyms

frame rate cap

Abbreviations

CAP

  • Input Latency
