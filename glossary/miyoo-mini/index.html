Miyoo Mini

A compact retro handheld for classic game emulation.

In detail

Miyoo Mini is a small handheld gaming device designed for retro emulation. It runs a lightweight frontend with RetroArch cores and supports a microSD card for firmware, ROMs, BIOS files, and saves.

Synonyms

Miyoo handheld, Mini
