Multisample Anti-Aliasing

Geometry-based AA sampling multiple times per pixel.

In detail

MSAA reduces edge aliasing by sampling polygon edges multiple times per pixel. It has better clarity than post-process AA but costs more performance and VRAM.

Synonyms

msaa

Abbreviations

MSAA

Related terms

  • Anti-Aliasing
  • Temporal Anti-Aliasing
  • Subpixel Morphological Anti-Aliasing
