Wi‑Fi 6E

Extension of Wi‑Fi 6 into the 6 GHz band with more spectrum.

In detail

Wi‑Fi 6E uses the 6 GHz band to provide many clean channels with low interference. Support depends on devices and region, but 6E routers can relieve congestion for 2.4 and 5 GHz networks used by the Steam Deck.

Synonyms

6 ghz wi-fi

Abbreviations

NA
