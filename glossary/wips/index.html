Wireless Intrusion Prevention System

System that actively blocks wireless threats.

In detail

WIPS enforces countermeasures like containment and frame protection. In Wi‑Fi 7, it must coordinate across bands and links to remain effective.

Synonyms

wireless IPS

Abbreviations
