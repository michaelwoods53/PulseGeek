LCD

A display tech that uses a backlight for brightness.

In detail

LCD displays rely on a constant backlight, which can use more power than OLED in dark content. Battery results on Steam Deck LCD differ from OLED at the same brightness.

Synonyms

LED‑lit LCD, liquid crystal display

Abbreviations

LCD
