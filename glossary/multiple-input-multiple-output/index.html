Multiple-Input Multiple-Output

Using multiple antennas to increase throughput and reliability.

In detail

MIMO exploits spatial diversity and multiplexing with multiple transmit and receive antennas. It enables multiple spatial streams, beamforming, and MU‑MIMO in Wi‑Fi.

Synonyms

multi-antenna

Abbreviations

MIMO
