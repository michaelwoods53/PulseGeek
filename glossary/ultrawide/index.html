Ultrawide

A monitor wider than 16:9, often 21:9 or 32:9 for immersive viewing.

In detail

Ultrawide monitors offer more horizontal space. Some games on Steam Deck support these ratios natively; others may require mods or accept letterboxing.

Synonyms

21:9 monitor, 3440x1440 display

Abbreviations

UW
