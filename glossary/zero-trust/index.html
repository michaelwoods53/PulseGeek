Zero Trust

A security model that verifies explicitly and limits implicit trust.

In detail

Zero Trust requires continuous verification of identity, device posture, and context. For Wi‑Fi IoT, it combines strong authentication, segmentation, and monitoring to reduce risk.

Synonyms

zero trust architecture
