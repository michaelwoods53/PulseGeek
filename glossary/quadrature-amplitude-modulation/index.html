Quadrature Amplitude Modulation

A modulation that combines amplitude changes on two quadrature carriers.

In detail

Quadrature Amplitude Modulation encodes bits by varying the amplitude of two carriers 90 degrees apart, forming constellation points. Higher‑order QAM increases bits per symbol but requires higher SNR and tighter EVM.

Synonyms

qam

Abbreviations

QAM

Related terms

  • Error Vector Magnitude (EVM)
  • Bit Error Rate (BER)
