Packet Capture

Recording frames for later decoding and analysis.

In detail

Packet capture is the process of collecting Wi‑Fi frames for analysis. It helps confirm whether puncturing masks are announced and respected by clients and APs.

Synonyms

pcap, frame capture

Abbreviations

PCAP
