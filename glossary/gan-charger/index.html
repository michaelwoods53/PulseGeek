Gallium Nitride Charger

A compact, efficient power adapter using GaN transistors.

In detail

GaN chargers run cooler and are smaller than silicon chargers. For Steam Deck, a quality GaN PD charger can deliver stable power while staying portable for travel.

Synonyms

GaN power adapter, GaN USB‑C charger

Abbreviations

GAN
