Dynamic Channel Assignment

Automatically picking channels based on measured conditions.

In detail

Dynamic Channel Assignment evaluates noise, utilization, and interference to select channels for access points, updating assignments as conditions change to maintain coexistence.

Synonyms

dca, automatic channel selection

Abbreviations

DCA
