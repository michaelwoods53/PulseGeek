Integer Scaling

Scaling that multiplies pixels by whole numbers.

In detail

Integer scaling preserves the original pixel grid without blurring, ideal for low-resolution content. It may create borders if the display does not divide evenly.

Synonyms

pixel-perfect scaling

Related terms

  • Nearest Neighbor
