Ricochet Anti-Cheat

An anti-cheat system used by Call of Duty that includes kernel-level components on Windows.

In detail

Ricochet Anti-Cheat blends server analytics with client modules, including kernel-level drivers on Windows. On Steam Deck, compatibility depends on game support paths and whether non-Windows clients are permitted.
