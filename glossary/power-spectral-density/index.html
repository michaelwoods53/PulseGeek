Power Spectral Density

Power distributed across frequency over channel bandwidth.

In detail

Power Spectral Density describes how transmit power is spread across frequency, influencing interference risk, channel bonding choices, and allowable power settings in shared bands.

Synonyms

psd, spectral density

Abbreviations

PSD
