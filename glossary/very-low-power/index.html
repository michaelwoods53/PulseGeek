Very Low Power

A 6 GHz device class for wearables and portable devices with very low EIRP.

In detail

Very Low Power allows on-the-go 6 GHz use with minimal interference risk. VLP devices have tight EIRP limits and are intended for short-range personal or IoT connections.

Synonyms

portable low power
