Video RAM

Memory on the GPU used for graphics data.

In detail

VRAM holds textures, frame buffers, and other GPU data. Running out of VRAM causes stutter, hitching, or texture pop-in when settings are too high.

Synonyms

graphics memory

Abbreviations

VRAM

Related terms

  • Frames Per Second
