MOS

Score representing perceived voice quality.

In detail

MOS is a standardized metric to rate voice quality impacted by latency, jitter, and loss. It can be tied to Wi‑Fi SLAs for collaboration apps.

Synonyms

mean opinion score, voice quality score

Abbreviations

MOS

Related terms

  • Latency
  • Jitter
  • Packet Loss
