CCMP-128

Baseline AES encryption mode for Wi‑Fi.

In detail

CCMP‑128, based on AES in Counter with CBC‑MAC mode, is widely deployed for WPA2 and WPA3 networks. It provides robust confidentiality and integrity for most enterprise use cases.

AES-CCMP
