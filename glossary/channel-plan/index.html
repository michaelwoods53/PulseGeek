Channel Plan

A layout of channels to minimize overlap and interference.

In detail

A channel plan assigns specific channels and widths to APs to reduce ACI and CCI. Effective plans consider DFS, regulatory domains, and building layout.

Synonyms

channel planning

Abbreviations

CHPLAN
