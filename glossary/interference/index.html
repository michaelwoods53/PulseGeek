Interference

Unwanted signals that disrupt Wi‑Fi communication.

In detail

Interference comes from neighboring networks and household devices like microwaves or Bluetooth. Reducing interference improves Steam Deck stability, throughput, and stream quality.

Synonyms

rf noise, wireless interference

Abbreviations

NA

Related terms

  • Channel Overlap
  • Signal-to-Noise Ratio
  • 2.4 GHz Band
