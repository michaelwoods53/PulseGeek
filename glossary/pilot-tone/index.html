Pilot Tone

Known reference symbols used for channel tracking.

In detail

Pilot tones are known subcarriers scattered through OFDM symbols, enabling channel estimation, phase tracking, and demodulation under time and frequency selectivity.

Synonyms

reference subcarrier

Abbreviations

PILOT

Related terms

  • Orthogonal Frequency-Division Multiplexing
  • Resource Unit
