802.1X

Framework for authenticating devices to a network.

In detail

802.1X controls access to wired and wireless networks using EAP over LAN and a RADIUS server. It underpins identity‑based policies for Wi‑Fi 7.

Synonyms

port‑based network access control

Abbreviations

8021X

Related terms

  • RADIUS
  • EAP‑TLS
  • NAC
