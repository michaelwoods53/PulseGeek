Moonlight

An open-source streaming client compatible with Sunshine hosts.

In detail

Moonlight is a free, open-source client that receives video streams from Sunshine or legacy GameStream-compatible hosts. It’s popular for low-latency streaming to devices like the Steam Deck, supporting modern codecs and control options.
