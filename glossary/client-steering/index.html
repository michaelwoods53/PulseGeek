Client Steering

Techniques that guide clients to the best band or AP.

In detail

Client steering nudges devices toward less congested bands or closer APs, improving balance across the network. In stadiums, it helps keep 6 GHz utilization high and prevents overload on legacy bands.

Synonyms

band steering

Abbreviations

STEER
