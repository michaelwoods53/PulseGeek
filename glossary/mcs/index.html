MCS

The modulation and coding level chosen for transmission.

In detail

MCS indexes define modulation depth and coding rates. With punctured channels, attainable MCS can change as interference removes subcarriers and alters SNR across the usable spectrum.

Synonyms

modulation and coding scheme

Abbreviations

MCS
