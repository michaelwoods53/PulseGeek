Airtime Utilization

Percentage of time the medium is busy.

In detail

Airtime utilization measures how much of the RF medium is occupied by transmissions and overhead. High utilization indicates contention and reduced throughput, guiding capacity and channel planning decisions.

Synonyms

channel utilization, airtime usage

Abbreviations

ATU

Related terms

  • Contention
