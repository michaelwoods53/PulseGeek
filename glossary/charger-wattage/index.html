Charger Wattage

The maximum power a charger can deliver, measured in watts.

In detail

Charger wattage affects whether the Steam Deck charges while gaming through a dock. Insufficient power can lead to battery drain or performance throttling.

Synonyms

charger power, PD wattage

Abbreviations

WATT
