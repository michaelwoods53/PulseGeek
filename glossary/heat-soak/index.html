Heat Soak

Build-up of heat over time in components and chassis.

In detail

Heat soak happens when prolonged load warms components and the enclosure, raising steady-state temperatures. Lowering power and smoothing frame times helps limit heat soak on long sessions.

Synonyms

thermal saturation, accumulated heat

Abbreviations

HS

Related terms

  • Ambient Temperature
  • Thermal Throttling
