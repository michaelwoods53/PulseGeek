Multi-Link Operation (MLO)

A method that lets devices use multiple links at once for more reliability and speed.

In detail

Multi-Link Operation allows a client and access point to establish and use multiple links concurrently across bands or channels. In dense venues, MLO can improve reliability by switching traffic to less congested links and reduce latency for real-time apps.

Synonyms

multi-link wi-fi, mlo

Abbreviations

MLO
