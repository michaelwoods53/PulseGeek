2.4 GHz

A longer-range Wi‑Fi band that is more prone to interference.

In detail

The 2.4 GHz band penetrates walls better but may be congested from many devices. It can be more stable at distance but slower for streaming.

Synonyms

2.4g band

Abbreviations

2.4G
