Smart Queue Management (SQM)

Algorithms that keep queues short to reduce latency.

In detail

SQM uses techniques like shaping and fairness to prevent queues from building up at bottlenecks. It helps avoid bufferbloat and keeps ping stable during heavy use.

Synonyms

queue management, active queue control

Abbreviations

SQM

Related terms

  • CAKE
  • FQ-CoDel
  • Bufferbloat
