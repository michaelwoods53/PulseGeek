MicroSD

Removable storage used to expand Steam Deck capacity.

In detail

A microSD card stores games and apps on Steam Deck. Users can format it as ext4, btrfs, or exFAT depending on needs.

Synonyms

SD card

Related terms

  • exFAT
  • Ext4
  • Btrfs
