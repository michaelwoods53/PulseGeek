OFDMA

A multi‑user method that splits channels into resource units.

In detail

OFDMA enables simultaneous transmissions by dividing a channel into multiple resource units. Under puncturing, the scheduler must map RUs to the remaining clean spectrum to maintain efficiency.

Synonyms

orthogonal frequency-division multiple access

Abbreviations

OFDMA

Related terms

  • Resource Unit (RU)
  • Scheduler
