Narrowband Interferer

A signal occupying a small slice of spectrum that disrupts subcarriers.

In detail

Narrowband interferers include devices like cordless phones or poorly filtered transmitters. Puncturing can mask the affected slice, preserving most of the channel’s capacity.

Synonyms

nbi, tone interferer

Abbreviations

NBI
