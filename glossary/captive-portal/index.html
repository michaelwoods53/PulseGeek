Captive Portal

A web page that controls guest access to Wi‑Fi.

In detail

A captive portal is a login or splash page used to authenticate and inform guests before granting internet access. In venues, it must be fast, mobile-friendly, and integrated with Passpoint or identity methods to reduce friction.

Synonyms

guest wifi login

Abbreviations

PORTAL
