CAKE

An advanced SQM algorithm that fairly shares bandwidth and trims latency.

In detail

CAKE builds on fq_codel with features like per-host fairness and ack filtering. It is popular on OpenWrt for keeping gaming latency low under load.

Synonyms

cake sqm

Related terms

  • SQM
  • fq_codel
  • OpenWrt
