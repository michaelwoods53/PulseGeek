TR‑398

Broadband Forum test specification for Wi‑Fi device performance.

In detail

TR‑398 defines standardized test cases for Wi‑Fi device performance such as throughput, latency, and coverage. It is often mapped to certification and vendor QA for Wi‑Fi 7.

Synonyms

BBF TR‑398, Wi‑Fi performance test

Abbreviations

TR398
