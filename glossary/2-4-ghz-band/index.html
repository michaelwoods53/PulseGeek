2.4 GHz Band

A Wi‑Fi band with longer range and more interference risk.

In detail

The 2.4 GHz band offers wider coverage and better wall penetration but has fewer non-overlapping channels and more interference from household devices. It can be useful for range on the Steam Deck but may have more congestion.

Synonyms

2.4g band, lower wifi band

Abbreviations

NA
