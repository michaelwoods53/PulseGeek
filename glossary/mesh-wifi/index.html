Mesh Wi‑Fi

Multiple APs that work together to cover a home.

In detail

Mesh systems spread coverage with several nodes. With Ethernet backhaul and proper channel planning, Wi‑Fi 7 mesh can keep gaming latency consistent across rooms.

Synonyms

mesh system, whole-home Wi‑Fi

Abbreviations

MESH
