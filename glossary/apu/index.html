APU

A chip that combines CPU and GPU on one package.

In detail

An APU integrates CPU and GPU on one package, improving efficiency in compact devices like the Steam Deck. Managing APU power limits and clocks directly affects temperature, throttling, and fan noise.

Synonyms

accelerated processing unit, amd apu

Abbreviations

APU
