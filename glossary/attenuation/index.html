Attenuation

The reduction of sound level with distance or other factors.

In detail

Attenuation curves shape how audio levels fall off with distance, angle, or occlusion. Proper tuning preserves clarity and prevents sudden drops or unrealistic loudness changes.

Synonyms

distance attenuation

Abbreviations

ATTN

Related terms

  • Spatialization
  • Occlusion
  • Distance-Based Filtering
