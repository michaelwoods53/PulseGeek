aptX Adaptive

A variable bitrate aptX codec tuned for stability and latency.

In detail

aptX Adaptive adjusts bitrate and error protection to balance audio quality, latency, and RF conditions. It can improve gaming responsiveness on the Steam Deck when both dongle and headset support it.

Synonyms

aptX Adaptive Low Latency

Abbreviations

APTX ADAPTIVE
