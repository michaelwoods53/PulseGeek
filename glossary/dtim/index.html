DTIM

A beacon field that signals buffered multicast and broadcast data.

In detail

The DTIM period tells clients when multicast and broadcast traffic will be sent. Tuning DTIM helps reduce unnecessary wakeups for sleeping stations, which is critical for IoT battery life.

Synonyms

delivery traffic indication message, beacon dtim

Abbreviations

DTIM

Related terms

  • Beacon Interval
  • Power Save Mode
  • Proxy ARP
