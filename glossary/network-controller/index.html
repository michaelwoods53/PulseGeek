Network Controller

A management platform for configuring and monitoring Wi‑Fi networks.

In detail

Network controllers provide centralized policy, telemetry, and automation. For 320 MHz deployments, controllers visualize utilization, trigger alerts, and tune RRM to maintain stability.

Synonyms

cloud controller, wifi controller
