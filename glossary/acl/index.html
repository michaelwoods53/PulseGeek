Access Control List

Rules that permit or deny traffic based on criteria.

In detail

ACLs restrict traffic by IP, port, or protocol to enforce least privilege. They complement VLANs and identity roles in Wi‑Fi 7 security designs.

Synonyms

traffic filter rules

Abbreviations

ACL

Related terms

  • VLAN
  • NAC
  • Microsegmentation
