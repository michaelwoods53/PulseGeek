Spectrum Analyzer

A tool that shows power over frequency to spot interference.

In detail

A spectrum analyzer visualizes RF energy across frequencies, revealing narrowband interferers or radar that may drive puncturing decisions. It is key for creating before/after utilization visuals.

Synonyms

rf analyzer, spectrum tool

Abbreviations

SA
