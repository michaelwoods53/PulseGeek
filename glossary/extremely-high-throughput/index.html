Extremely High Throughput

Capability class in 802.11be targeting very high data rates and efficiency.

In detail

Extremely High Throughput (EHT) is the capability class introduced by 802.11be. It encompasses PHY and MAC enhancements that increase throughput, reduce latency, and improve spectrum use compared to previous Wi‑Fi generations.

Synonyms

EHT, High throughput Wi‑Fi

Abbreviations

EHT
