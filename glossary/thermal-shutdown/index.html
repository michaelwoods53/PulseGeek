Thermal Shutdown

Automatic power-off to prevent heat damage.

In detail

Thermal shutdown occurs when temperatures exceed safety limits. If a Steam Deck shuts down under load, review ambient conditions, power limits, and fan settings to prevent repeats.

Synonyms

overheat shutdown, high temp shutdown

Abbreviations

TS

Related terms

  • Tjunction
  • Thermal Throttling
  • Heat Soak
