Rogue Access Point

Unauthorized AP connected to or imitating your network.

In detail

A rogue access point may be accidentally or maliciously installed and can bypass security policies. Detection uses RF scans, wired correlation, and policy enforcement via WIPS and NAC.

Synonyms

rogue AP

Abbreviations

RAP

Related terms
