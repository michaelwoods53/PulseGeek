HSP

Legacy Bluetooth profile for simple headset audio and mic.

In detail

HSP is an older profile used for basic headset audio and microphone functions. It offers limited quality compared to A2DP for music and is often replaced by HFP for modern devices.

Synonyms

Headset Profile

Abbreviations

HSP
