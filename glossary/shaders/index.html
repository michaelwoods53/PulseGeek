Shaders

GPU effects that change how the game image looks.

In detail

Shaders simulate effects like CRT scanlines or smoothing. On Miyoo, heavy shaders may reduce performance, so simple passes or overlays are often preferred for consistent framerate.

Synonyms

RA shaders, CRT shaders

Abbreviations

SHDR

Related terms

  • Overlays
  • Integer Scaling
