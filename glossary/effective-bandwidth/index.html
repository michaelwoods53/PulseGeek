Effective Bandwidth

The portion of a configured channel that is actually usable.

In detail

Effective bandwidth is the practical throughput‑carrying portion of a channel after accounting for interference and protocol overhead. Puncturing aims to maximize effective bandwidth by masking bad slices.

Synonyms

usable bandwidth

Abbreviations

EBW
