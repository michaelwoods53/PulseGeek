Fragmentation

Splitting packets into smaller pieces due to MTU limits.

In detail

When packets exceed the path MTU, they are fragmented, which adds overhead and can reduce performance. Avoiding fragmentation helps the Steam Deck maintain steady throughput and lower latency.

Synonyms

packet fragmentation

Abbreviations

NA

Related terms

  • Maximum Transmission Unit
  • Packet Loss
  • Latency
