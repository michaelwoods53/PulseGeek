Artwork Thumbnails

Images shown in the game browser UI.

In detail

Artwork thumbnails are images like box art or screenshots attached to ROM entries. Missing or broken thumbnails on Onion OS often trace to wrong paths or cache issues.

Synonyms

box art, game thumbnails

Abbreviations

ART
