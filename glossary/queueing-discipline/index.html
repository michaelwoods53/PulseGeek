Queueing Discipline

Algorithms that decide packet service order.

In detail

Queueing disciplines such as FIFO, FQ‑CoDel, and WFQ shape how packets are buffered and transmitted, impacting latency and jitter especially under load.

Synonyms

qdisc, scheduler

Abbreviations

QDISC
