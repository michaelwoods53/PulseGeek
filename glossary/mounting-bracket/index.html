Mounting Bracket

Hardware used to affix APs to ceilings or walls.

In detail

Mounting brackets help position APs securely and orient antennas correctly. The right bracket maintains ideal azimuth and elevation for planned coverage.

Synonyms

ap mount

Abbreviations
