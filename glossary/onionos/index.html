OnionOS

A community firmware for Miyoo devices with RetroArch integration.

In detail

OnionOS is a popular community firmware for Miyoo Mini models. It includes RetroArch, curated app menus, and quality-of-life features like better directory structure, hotkey management, and easy updating.
