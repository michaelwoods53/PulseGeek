Audio routing

Selecting which devices handle output and input audio.

In detail

Audio routing assigns music, game sound, and voice chat to specific outputs and microphones. On the Steam Deck, PipeWire and system settings control routing between speakers, Bluetooth, USB, or HDMI.

Synonyms

device mapping, audio path

Related terms

  • PipeWire
  • HFP
