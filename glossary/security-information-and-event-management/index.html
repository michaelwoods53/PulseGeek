Security Information and Event Management

Software that collects and correlates security events.

In detail

SIEM platforms aggregate logs and alerts from many sources to support detection and investigations. AI can score and cluster events, reduce noise, and highlight suspicious behaviors for analysts.

Synonyms

SIEM platform

Abbreviations
