Scheduler

Logic that assigns transmissions, RUs, and priorities to clients.

In detail

The scheduler coordinates when and how clients transmit or receive. With puncturing, it must account for masked sub‑channels, QoS priorities, and fairness to maintain stable performance.

Synonyms

mac scheduler, ap scheduler

Abbreviations

SCH

Related terms

  • OFDMA
  • Airtime Fairness
  • QoS
