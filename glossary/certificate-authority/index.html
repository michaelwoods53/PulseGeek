Certificate Authority

Entity that issues digital certificates used in EAP‑TLS.

In detail

A Certificate Authority validates identities and issues certificates to users and devices. Strong Wi‑Fi authentication relies on a well‑managed CA with secure key storage, revocation, and automated lifecycle management.
