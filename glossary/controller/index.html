Controller

A system that manages APs, policies, and RF settings.

In detail

A WLAN controller centralizes configuration, firmware, and RF policy for many access points. It exposes scheduler parameters, QoS mapping, and telemetry needed to tune OFDMA behavior.

Synonyms

wlan controller

Abbreviations

WLC

Related terms

  • Access Point
  • Scheduler
  • QoS
