TXOP

A bounded time interval to transmit frames once the medium is won.

In detail

A transmit opportunity is a time window during which a station can send one or more frames. In OFDMA, TXOP budgeting interacts with RU assignments and QoS rules to satisfy latency and throughput targets.

Synonyms

transmit opportunity
