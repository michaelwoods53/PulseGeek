Fiber Uplink

A high-speed optical connection from edge devices to the core.

In detail

Fiber uplinks provide high bandwidth and low latency backhaul from access switches to aggregation or core layers. In stadiums, fiber helps handle peak traffic from dense AP clusters.

Synonyms

optical uplink

Abbreviations

FIBER

Related terms

  • PoE
  • Switching
  • Backhaul
