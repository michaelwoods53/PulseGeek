USB-C

A reversible connector that carries data, video (Alt Mode), and power.

In detail

USB-C is a connector that can transmit data, video via DisplayPort Alt Mode, and power. On the Steam Deck, it enables docking with HDMI or DisplayPort outputs while charging through power delivery.

Synonyms

Type-C, USB Type-C

Abbreviations

USBC

Related terms

  • Alt Mode
  • Power Delivery
  • DisplayPort
