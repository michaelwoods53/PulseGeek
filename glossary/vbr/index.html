VBR

Encoding mode that adjusts bitrate to content.

In detail

Variable bitrate allocates more bits to complex scenes and fewer to simple ones. It can improve quality per bit but risks latency spikes without careful buffering.

Synonyms

variable bitrate

Abbreviations

VBR

Related terms

  • CBR
  • Dynamic Bitrate
