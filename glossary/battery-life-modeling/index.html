Battery Life Modeling

Estimating device runtime from workload and power metrics.

In detail

Battery life modeling uses inputs like radio on‑time, packet sizes, and sleep currents to forecast how long a device will run. It validates whether Wi‑Fi power profiles meet product goals.

Synonyms

battery estimation, battery life calculator

Abbreviations
