Automated Frequency Coordination (AFC)

A system that manages 6 GHz outdoor use to avoid interference with incumbents.

In detail

AFC is a spectrum management system for standard-power 6 GHz devices, typically outdoors. It approves channels and power levels to protect incumbent users. Stadiums with outdoor concourses may rely on AFC to safely use 6 GHz at higher power.

Synonyms

afc system, 6 ghz afc

Abbreviations

AFC
