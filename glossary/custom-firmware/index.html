Custom Firmware

Non-stock system software installed on a device.

In detail

Custom firmware replaces or extends the device’s stock OS with new features. Onion OS is a custom firmware focused on emulation usability and performance.

Synonyms

cfw, third-party firmware

Abbreviations

CFW
