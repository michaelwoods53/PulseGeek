LDAC

A high-bitrate Bluetooth codec focused on audio quality.

In detail

LDAC is a Sony codec that can deliver higher bitrates over Bluetooth. On the Steam Deck, LDAC may improve music fidelity but can have higher latency than gaming-focused codecs.

Synonyms

Sony LDAC

Abbreviations

LDAC
