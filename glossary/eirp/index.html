Equivalent Isotropically Radiated Power

The total radiated power in a given direction from an antenna system.

In detail

EIRP combines transmitter power, cable losses, and antenna gain to describe effective radiated power. Proper EIRP management in arenas prevents interference and meets regulatory limits.

Synonyms

effective radiated power

Abbreviations
