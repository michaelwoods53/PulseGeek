Wi‑Fi

Wireless networking used by the Steam Deck for streaming and downloads.

In detail

Wi‑Fi enables the Steam Deck to stream to TVs and download games. Using 5GHz bands and proper router placement reduces latency for living room play.

Synonyms

wireless network, 5GHz Wi‑Fi

Abbreviations

WIFI
