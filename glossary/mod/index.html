Mod

A user-created change to a game’s content or behavior.

In detail

A mod is any user-created addition or change that alters a game’s content, visuals, audio, UI, or gameplay. Mods can be simple texture swaps or complex plugins and scripts that add new features or fix issues.

Synonyms

modification, game mod
