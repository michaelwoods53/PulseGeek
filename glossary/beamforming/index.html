Beamforming

Directing signal energy toward clients using antenna arrays.

In detail

Beamforming improves SNR at the receiver by steering energy. In punctured channels, beamforming can help maintain higher MCS on the remaining clean subcarriers.

Synonyms

tx beamforming

Abbreviations

BF
