Coil Whine

High-pitched noise from vibrating power components.

In detail

Coil whine is an electrical noise from inductors under certain loads. It’s not harmful but can be annoying; adjusting frame caps and power draw sometimes changes its intensity.

Synonyms

electrical whine, inductor noise

Abbreviations

CW
