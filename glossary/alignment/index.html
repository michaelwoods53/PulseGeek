Alignment

A flocking force that matches the agent’s direction to neighbors.

In detail

Alignment averages neighbor velocities to set a desired heading, promoting smooth group movement. Together with cohesion and separation, it stabilizes flock motion.

Synonyms

heading alignment

Related terms

  • Cohesion
