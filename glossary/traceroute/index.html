Traceroute

A diagnostic that shows the path and delay to a destination.

In detail

Traceroute lists the hops between your device and a server, helping identify where latency or loss occurs along the route, such as ISP or peering points.

Synonyms

trace route, tracert

Related terms

  • Ping
  • BGP
  • Peering
