Battery Wear

The reduction in maximum charge capacity over time.

In detail

Battery wear indicates how much capacity has been lost from the original design. Limiting heat, avoiding constant 100% charging, and using efficient settings can slow wear on Steam Deck.

Synonyms

capacity loss, degradation

Abbreviations

BW

Related terms

  • Cycle Count
  • Thermal Throttling
