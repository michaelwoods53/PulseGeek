High Dynamic Range

A video feature that expands color and brightness for richer images.

In detail

HDR increases brightness range and color gamut. On Steam Deck, HDR behavior depends on TV/monitor support and software capabilities, which can limit or disable HDR in some cases.

Synonyms

HDR video, HDR gaming

Abbreviations

HDR
