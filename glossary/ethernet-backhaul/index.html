Ethernet Backhaul

A wired link between mesh nodes or APs.

In detail

Ethernet backhaul removes wireless hop latency and frees airtime, making mesh networks more stable for gaming and streaming throughout the home.

Synonyms

wired backhaul, LAN backhaul

Abbreviations

EB
