EDCA

A QoS method using access categories with different contention settings.

In detail

EDCA defines queues with distinct contention windows and TXOP limits to prioritize traffic types like voice or video. With OFDMA, EDCA priorities can influence RU scheduling and trigger cadence.

Synonyms

enhanced distributed channel access

Abbreviations

EDCA
