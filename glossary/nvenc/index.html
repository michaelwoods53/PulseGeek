NVENC

NVIDIA’s GPU-based video encoder used for real-time streaming.

In detail

NVENC offloads video encoding to NVIDIA GPUs, freeing the CPU and reducing latency. It supports modern codecs like HEVC and AV1 on newer cards, improving stream quality for the Steam Deck.

Synonyms

NVIDIA hardware encoder

Abbreviations

NVENC

Related terms

  • Quick Sync
  • VCE
  • AV1
