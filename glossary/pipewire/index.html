PipeWire

A Linux multimedia server managing audio and video streams.

In detail

PipeWire handles audio devices, streams, and routing on modern Linux systems including SteamOS. It replaces or works with PulseAudio to connect apps to Bluetooth and other devices.

Synonyms

Linux audio server
