Partition

A logical section of a storage device with its own filesystem.

In detail

A partition splits a physical storage device into one or more logical sections. On Steam Deck, a microSD can be partitioned to separate Steam libraries from emulation or media data.

Synonyms

disk partition

Abbreviations

PART
