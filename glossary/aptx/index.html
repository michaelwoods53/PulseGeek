aptX

A family of Bluetooth codecs designed for higher quality and lower latency.

In detail

aptX and its variants (LL and Adaptive) are codecs from Qualcomm. When supported end to end, they can reduce latency for gaming on the Steam Deck compared to SBC or AAC.

Synonyms

Qualcomm aptX

Abbreviations

APTX

Related terms

  • A2DP
  • aptX Adaptive
