Anti-Aliasing

Methods that smooth jagged edges in games.

In detail

Anti-aliasing techniques reduce jagged edges and shimmer. Options range from post-process methods to multi-sampling, each with unique performance and clarity trade-offs.

Synonyms

anti aliasing

Abbreviations

AA

Related terms

  • Temporal Anti-Aliasing
  • Fast Approximate Anti-Aliasing
  • Multisample Anti-Aliasing
