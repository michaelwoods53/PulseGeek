Signal-to-Noise Ratio

The strength of the Wi‑Fi signal compared to background noise.

In detail

Signal-to-Noise Ratio measures how clearly a Wi‑Fi signal stands out from noise and interference. Higher SNR improves throughput and stability, leading to smoother Steam Deck gameplay and streaming.

Synonyms

snr, signal quality

Abbreviations

SNR

Related terms

  • Received Signal Strength Indicator
  • Interference
  • Channel Overlap
