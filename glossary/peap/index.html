Protected Extensible Authentication Protocol

A tunneled EAP method commonly used with passwords.

In detail

PEAP creates a TLS tunnel to protect inner authentication, often using MSCHAPv2. It is widely supported but less secure than certificate-based methods like EAP‑TLS for large public venues.

Synonyms

peap-mschapv2

Abbreviations

PEAP
