Seating Bowl

The main spectator seating area in a stadium.

In detail

The seating bowl is the densest spectator area and usually the hardest Wi‑Fi zone. It requires careful sectorization, mounting, and channel planning to meet peak throughput and latency goals.

Synonyms

bowl seating
