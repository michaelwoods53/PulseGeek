QoS

Network rules that prioritize certain traffic.

In detail

QoS marks and schedules packets so time‑sensitive data, like cloud gaming inputs and frames, get priority over bulk traffic to reduce latency and jitter.

Synonyms

quality of service

Abbreviations

QOS
