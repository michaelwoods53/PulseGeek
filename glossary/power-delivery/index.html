Power Delivery

A USB-C standard for negotiated charging up to higher wattages.

In detail

Power Delivery allows a USB-C charger to negotiate power with the Steam Deck and dock. Proper PD wattage helps maintain battery levels and performance during docked gameplay.

Synonyms

USB PD, PD charging

Abbreviations

PD

  • USB-C
  • Docking Station
