Quality of Service

Network rules that prioritize or shape traffic to manage latency.

In detail

Quality of Service classifies and prioritizes traffic so latency-sensitive packets receive faster handling. Proper QoS can reduce spikes during busy household usage.

Synonyms

traffic prioritization

Abbreviations

QoS

Related terms

  • SQM
  • Bufferbloat
  • MTU
