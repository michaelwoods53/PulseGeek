Over‑the‑Air Testing

Testing conducted via radiated signals in controlled environments.

In detail

OTA testing uses chambers and antennas to emulate real wireless conditions. For Wi‑Fi 7, OTA validates beamforming, MIMO, and 6 GHz performance against standard requirements.

Synonyms

OTA testing, Chamber testing

Abbreviations

OTA
