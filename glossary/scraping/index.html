Scraping

The process of fetching game info and images online.

In detail

Scraping pulls titles, box art, and descriptions from databases. On Onion OS, accurate ROM names and stable internet are essential for reliable results.

Synonyms

metadata scraping, artwork scraping

Abbreviations
