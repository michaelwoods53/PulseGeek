6 GHz

A Wi‑Fi band offering large channel capacity and less interference.

In detail

The 6 GHz band provides wide, contiguous spectrum for Wi‑Fi, enabling more channels and wider channel widths. In stadiums, it reduces congestion and supports high-throughput, low-latency applications when paired with careful channel planning.

Synonyms

wi-fi 6e band, u-nii-5-to-8

Abbreviations

6GHZ
