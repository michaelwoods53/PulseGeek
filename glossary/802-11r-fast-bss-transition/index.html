Fast BSS Transition

Mechanism to speed secure roaming between APs.

In detail

802.11r streamlines key exchanges for faster roaming. With Wi‑Fi 7, it must be configured alongside WPA3 and PMF to maintain strong security.

Synonyms

802.11r

Abbreviations

11R
