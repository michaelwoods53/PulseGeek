WMM

A Wi‑Fi QoS profile mapping traffic into four access categories.

In detail

Wi‑Fi Multimedia maps traffic into voice, video, best effort, and background categories. When combined with OFDMA, WMM categories can guide RU prioritization and scheduling intervals for latency-sensitive flows.
