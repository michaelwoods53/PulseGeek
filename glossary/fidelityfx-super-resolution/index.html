FidelityFX Super Resolution

AMD’s open upscaling technology.

In detail

FSR upscales lower-resolution frames to higher output resolutions using spatial or temporal methods, aiming to improve performance on many GPUs and platforms.

Synonyms

amd fsr

Abbreviations

FSR
