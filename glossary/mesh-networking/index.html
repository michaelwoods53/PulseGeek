Mesh Networking

A network where nodes relay traffic for each other.

In detail

Mesh networking connects APs wirelessly to extend coverage without cabling. Antenna selection determines backhaul reliability and client performance in mesh designs.

Synonyms

wireless mesh

Abbreviations

MESH

Related terms
