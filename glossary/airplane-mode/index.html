Airplane Mode

A toggle that disables wireless radios to save power.

In detail

Airplane Mode disables Wi‑Fi and Bluetooth, cutting idle power drain. On Steam Deck, it is useful during travel or offline gaming sessions to extend battery life.

Synonyms

flight mode, offline mode

Abbreviations

AM

Related terms
