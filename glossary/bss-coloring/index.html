BSS Coloring

A method to distinguish overlapping networks to enable spatial reuse.

In detail

BSS coloring tags frames so devices can identify and potentially ignore transmissions from neighboring networks. Combined with puncturing, it helps reuse spectrum in dense deployments while managing interference.

Synonyms

spatial reuse coloring

Abbreviations

BSS
