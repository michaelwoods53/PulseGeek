WPA4

Emerging successor to WPA3 expected to bring stronger authentication and encryption.

In detail

WPA4 is the anticipated evolution of Wi‑Fi security beyond WPA3, aiming to refine authentication resilience, crypto agility, and management protections. Enterprises should monitor draft features to plan for compatibility and migration readiness.

Synonyms

Wi‑Fi Protected Access 4

Abbreviations

WPA4

Related terms

  • WPA3
  • EAP-TLS
