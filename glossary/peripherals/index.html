Peripherals

External devices like keyboards, mice, controllers, and headsets.

In detail

Peripherals connect through USB or Bluetooth to enhance docked Steam Deck use. They include keyboards, mice, headsets, controllers, and storage readers, often attached via a dock.

Synonyms

keyboard and mouse, controllers

Abbreviations

PRPH
