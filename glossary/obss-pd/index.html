OBSS-PD

A threshold that allows reuse when overlapping signals are weak.

In detail

Overlapping BSS Packet Detect raises the energy-detection threshold so a device can transmit despite distant co-channel activity. Proper OBSS-PD tuning can increase spatial reuse and improve OFDMA scheduling opportunities.

Synonyms

overlapping bss packet detect

Abbreviations
