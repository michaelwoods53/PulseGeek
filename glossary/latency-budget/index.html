Latency Budget

The maximum acceptable delay for an application’s data.

In detail

A latency budget defines how much delay a workload can tolerate. Power profiles trade latency for fewer wakeups; mapping a clear budget guides TWT intervals and batching size.

Synonyms

response time budget, latency envelope

Abbreviations

LB

Related terms

  • Target Wake Time
  • Quality of Service
  • Duty Cycle
