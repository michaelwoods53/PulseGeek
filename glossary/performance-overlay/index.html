Performance Overlay

Built-in HUD showing FPS, frame time, and system stats.

In detail

The SteamOS performance overlay is an on-screen display for FPS, frame time, temperatures, and power draw. It helps verify the impact of settings on heat and noise without external tools.

Synonyms

steamos performance overlay, steam deck overlay

Abbreviations

PO
