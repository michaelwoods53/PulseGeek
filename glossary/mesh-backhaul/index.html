Mesh Backhaul

Links connecting mesh nodes to the main network.

In detail

Mesh backhaul can use dedicated radios or shared channels. Planning backhaul paths and bands, especially 6 GHz, is vital to keep client airtime available.

Synonyms

wireless backhaul

Abbreviations

MBH

Related terms

  • Channel Bonding
  • MLO
  • Airtime Fairness
