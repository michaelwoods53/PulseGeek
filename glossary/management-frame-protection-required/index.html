Management Frame Protection Required

Mode that enforces PMF for all clients.

In detail

Setting PMF to required ensures only clients that support management frame protection can join. This blocks legacy devices that could weaken security and helps prevent deauth and disassociation attacks.

Synonyms

PMF required

Abbreviations

PMF-REQ

Related terms

  • Protected Management Frames
  • WPA3
