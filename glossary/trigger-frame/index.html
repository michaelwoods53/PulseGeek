Trigger Frame

A control frame that prompts clients to transmit in uplink OFDMA.

In detail

The Trigger Frame carries scheduling directives such as RU assignments, modulation and coding constraints, and timing. Clients respond with uplink data in their allocated RUs, enabling coordinated multi-user access and lower contention overhead.

Synonyms

trigger-based uplink

Abbreviations

TF

Related terms

  • OFDMA
  • Resource Unit
  • Buffer Status Report
