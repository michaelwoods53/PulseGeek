802.11be Extremely High Throughput

The IEEE spec that defines Wi‑Fi 7 features and limits.

In detail

802.11be EHT defines how Wi‑Fi 7 works across PHY and MAC layers, including wider channels, higher QAM, multi-RU, and MLO to improve capacity, reliability, and latency.

Synonyms

EHT, Wi‑Fi 7 standard

Abbreviations

EHT

Related terms

  • Wi‑Fi 7
  • Multi‑RU
  • 4096‑QAM
