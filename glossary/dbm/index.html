Decibel-milliwatts

A unit expressing power relative to 1 milliwatt.

In detail

dBm is used to state transmit power, received signal, and noise floor. It anchors link budget math and helps compare AP power settings and antenna gains.

Synonyms

dbm level

Abbreviations

DBM
