Wi‑Fi 7

The next‑gen Wi‑Fi standard with wider channels, multi‑link, and higher throughput.

In detail

Wi‑Fi 7 is 802.11be and introduces 320 MHz channels, multi‑link operation, and advanced modulation to greatly increase top speeds and reduce latency. It can improve performance for demanding apps and dense home networks.

Synonyms

802.11be

Abbreviations

WIFI7
