Wireless Intrusion Detection System

Tool that detects wireless threats and anomalies.

In detail

WIDS monitors RF and WLAN activity to flag attacks, misconfigurations, and rogue devices. For Wi‑Fi 7, it should parse new frames and multi‑link behaviors.

Synonyms

wireless IDS

Abbreviations

WIDS
