AV Receiver

A device that switches HDMI inputs and powers speakers for home theaters.

In detail

An AV receiver manages HDMI inputs, decodes audio, and outputs to speakers. In a Steam Deck setup, it can sit between the dock and TV while handling audio via ARC or eARC.

Synonyms

home theater receiver, AVR

Abbreviations

AVR
