Automated Frequency Coordination

A system that coordinates 6 GHz operation to avoid incumbents.

In detail

Automated Frequency Coordination is a cloud-based mechanism that authorizes standard-power 6 GHz access points on specific frequencies to protect incumbent services. APs query databases to obtain permitted channels and power levels.
