Counter Mode CBC-MAC Protocol

Default AES‑based cipher in WPA2 and WPA3.

In detail

CCMP uses AES in counter mode with CBC‑MAC for integrity. It remains widely used and compatible, though GCMP‑256 may be preferred for high‑assurance Wi‑Fi 7 scenarios.

Synonyms

AES‑CCMP

Abbreviations

CCMP

Related terms

  • WPA3
  • GCMP‑256
  • WPA2
