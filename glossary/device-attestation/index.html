Device Attestation

Proof that a device is genuine and in a trusted state.

In detail

Device attestation uses cryptographic proofs from secure hardware to verify identity and integrity. In Wi‑Fi IoT, it complements onboarding and limits rogue devices.

Synonyms

hardware attestation
