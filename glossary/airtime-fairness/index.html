Airtime Fairness

Allocating equal transmission time across clients for efficiency.

In detail

Airtime fairness policies prevent slower clients from dominating the channel. With punctured channels, ATF helps balance throughput and latency across clients using reduced but cleaner bandwidth.

Synonyms

airtime scheduling fairness

Abbreviations

ATF

Related terms

  • Scheduler
  • OFDMA
  • Resource Unit (RU)
