Wattage

Rate of energy use measured in watts.

In detail

Wattage indicates how much power the Steam Deck consumes. Lower wattage settings usually reduce temperatures and fan noise, improving comfort and battery life.

Synonyms

power draw, electrical power

Abbreviations

W
