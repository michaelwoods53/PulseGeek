Extended High Throughput

A feature set that enables higher efficiency and throughput for modern Wi‑Fi.

In detail

Extended High Throughput is a collection of mechanisms that boost Wi‑Fi performance and efficiency, including wider channels, enhanced OFDMA resource allocation, and better multi-user operations. It underpins capabilities like 320 MHz channels in the 6 GHz band.

Abbreviations

EHT
