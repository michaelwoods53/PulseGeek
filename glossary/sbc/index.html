SBC

Mandatory Bluetooth audio codec with broad compatibility.

In detail

SBC is the baseline codec used by A2DP for Bluetooth audio. It is widely supported on the Steam Deck but may have higher latency and lower efficiency than AAC, aptX, or LDAC.

Synonyms

Subband Codec

Abbreviations

SBC
