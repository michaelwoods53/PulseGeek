Channel Mask

A pattern that defines which sub‑channels are used or omitted.

In detail

A channel mask is a bit pattern or configuration that signals which resource units or sub‑channels are disabled due to interference or rules. In puncturing, the mask guides transmitters and receivers on the usable portions of a wide channel.

Synonyms

puncturing mask, masking pattern

Abbreviations

CM

Related terms

  • Preamble Puncturing
  • Resource Unit (RU)
  • Spectral Mask
