Screen Tearing

Visible split between frames on the display.

In detail

Screen tearing happens when frames and refresh rates are out of sync. VSync or VRR solutions like G-SYNC and FreeSync can minimize it.

Synonyms

tearing

Related terms

  • Vertical Sync
  • Variable Refresh Rate
  • Frames Per Second
