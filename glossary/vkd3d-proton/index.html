VKD3D-Proton

A Direct3D 12 to Vulkan translator used in Proton.

In detail

VKD3D-Proton provides a path for D3D12 titles to run on Vulkan. Stability and version updates can influence whether games reach the online menus reliably on Steam Deck.

Synonyms

D3D12 to Vulkan
