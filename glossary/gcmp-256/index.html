GCMP-256

High‑strength Wi‑Fi encryption and integrity algorithm.

In detail

GCMP‑256 provides authenticated encryption with a 256‑bit key, meeting strict enterprise and government requirements. It is available in WPA3 Enterprise 192‑bit mode for higher assurance deployments.

Synonyms

Galois/Counter Mode 256-bit

Abbreviations

GCMP-256

Related terms

  • WPA3
  • CCMP-128
