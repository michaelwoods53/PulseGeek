Bluetooth Coexistence

Strategies to reduce Wi‑Fi and Bluetooth mutual impact.

In detail

Bluetooth coexistence techniques coordinate traffic or separate frequencies to limit collisions between Bluetooth and Wi‑Fi, improving reliability for both technologies.

Synonyms

bt coexistence, bt-wifi coexistence

Abbreviations

BTC
