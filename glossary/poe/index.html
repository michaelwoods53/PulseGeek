PoE

Delivers power to APs over Ethernet cables.

In detail

Power over Ethernet supplies electrical power and data on the same cable. Higher‑class PoE may be needed for advanced WiFi 8 APs with multiple radios.

Synonyms

power over ethernet, 802.3at af bt

Abbreviations

POE
