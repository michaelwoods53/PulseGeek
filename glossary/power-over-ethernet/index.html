Power over Ethernet

Technology to power devices through Ethernet cabling.

In detail

PoE delivers electrical power alongside data, simplifying AP installation by removing the need for separate power lines. Planning must account for total power budgets and standards supported by switches and APs.

Synonyms

poe power

Abbreviations

PoE
