USB-C DAC

A USB-C device that converts digital audio to analog for wired headsets.

In detail

A USB-C DAC bypasses Bluetooth and sends clean, low-latency audio to wired headphones. It is a reliable alternative for the Steam Deck when wireless introduces delay.

Synonyms

USB audio adapter, digital to analog converter

Abbreviations

DAC
