Sharpening

Filter that boosts edge contrast for clarity.

In detail

Sharpening increases edge contrast to recover detail lost to AA or upscaling. Overuse can cause halos and noise.

Synonyms

image sharpening

Related terms

  • Temporal Anti-Aliasing
  • Image Reconstruction
  • Anisotropic Filtering
