Low Power Indoor

A 6 GHz device class limited to indoor use with capped EIRP.

In detail

Low Power Indoor defines access points and clients that operate indoors with restricted EIRP and require protective measures such as no outdoor antennas. LPI enables dense deployments without AFC.

Synonyms

indoor low power

Abbreviations

LPI

Related terms

  • Very Low Power
  • Standard Power Access Point
