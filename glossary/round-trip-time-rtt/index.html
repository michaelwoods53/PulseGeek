Round-Trip Time

The time it takes for a packet to go to a server and back.

In detail

RTT reflects connection responsiveness. Measuring RTT under load helps verify whether QoS and SQM settings on Wi‑Fi 7 are working for gaming.

Synonyms

RTT, ping time

Abbreviations

RTT
