External SSD

A solid-state drive connected externally via USB-C or a hub.

In detail

An external SSD offers higher speeds than microSD and can host Steam libraries. On Steam Deck, it complements microSD storage for large games or fast transfers.

Synonyms

portable ssd

Abbreviations

ESSD

Related terms
