Sightline

An unobstructed view path between points in a level.

In detail

Sightlines control what players and AI can see at a distance. Designers shape them with cover, elevation, and turns to support fairness, surprise, and navigation cues.

Synonyms

line of sight

Abbreviations

LOS

Related terms
