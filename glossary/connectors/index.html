Connectors

Hardware that joins cables to antennas and radios.

In detail

Common Wi‑Fi connectors include RP‑SMA and N‑type. The right connector minimizes loss, ensures weather sealing outdoors, and maintains impedance continuity.

Synonyms

rf connectors

Abbreviations

RF-CONN

Related terms

  • Cable Loss
