Polarization

The orientation of the electric field of a radio wave.

In detail

Polarization describes the orientation of the RF wave’s electric field, such as vertical, horizontal, or circular. Matching polarization between antennas improves link reliability and SNR.

Synonyms

antenna polarization, polarisation

Abbreviations

POL

Related terms

  • Cross-Polarization
  • Dual Polarization
