Roaming

The process of a client moving between APs while maintaining connectivity.

In detail

Roaming involves scanning, authentication, and re-association as clients move. Proper AP placement, thresholds, and fast transition mechanisms reduce interruptions and latency.

Synonyms

client handoff

Abbreviations

ROAM

Related terms

  • Received Signal Strength Indicator
  • 802.1X
  • Quality of Service
