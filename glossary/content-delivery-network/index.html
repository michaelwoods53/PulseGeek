Content Delivery Network

A distributed network that serves content from locations near users.

In detail

CDNs reduce distance and congestion by caching content at the edge. For gaming, CDNs often serve downloads and updates, while dedicated servers host matches.

Synonyms

edge network

Abbreviations

CDN

Related terms
