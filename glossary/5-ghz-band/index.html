5 GHz Band

A Wi‑Fi band with higher speeds and shorter range.

In detail

The 5 GHz band provides more channels, less interference, and higher throughput, but has shorter range. It is commonly preferred for Steam Deck gaming and streaming when you are closer to the access point.

Synonyms

5g band, higher wifi band

Abbreviations

NA
