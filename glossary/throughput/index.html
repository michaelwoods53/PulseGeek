Throughput

The volume of data delivered over time.

In detail

Throughput reflects effective payload data delivered, not just PHY rates. Schedulers balance throughput against fairness and latency by selecting RU sizes, MCS, and user groupings.

Synonyms

data rate

Abbreviations

TPUT
