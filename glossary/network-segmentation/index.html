Network Segmentation

Separating devices into policy domains on the network.

In detail

Segmentation isolates IoT devices by function or risk using VLANs, ACLs, or SSIDs. It limits blast radius, simplifies compliance, and pairs well with identity-based access.

Synonyms

VLAN segmentation

Related terms

  • RADIUS
  • Zero Trust
  • WPA3 Enterprise
