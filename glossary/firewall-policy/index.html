Firewall Policy

Rules that permit or deny traffic based on identity and context.

In detail

Firewall policies in Wi‑Fi environments should align with identity, device type, and application needs. Role‑based rules and app awareness support zero trust while preserving user experience.

Synonyms

access policy

Abbreviations

FWPOL
