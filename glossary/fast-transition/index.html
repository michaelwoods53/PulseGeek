Fast Transition

Standard for faster roaming by pre‑authenticating with target APs.

In detail

Fast Transition streamlines roaming by enabling key establishment before handoff, reducing packet loss and latency during AP changes in Wi‑Fi 7 deployments.

Synonyms

802.11r, FT roaming

Abbreviations

FT
