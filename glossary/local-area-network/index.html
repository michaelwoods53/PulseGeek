LAN

A local network that connects devices within a home or building.

In detail

A Local Area Network links devices in a limited area, often through a router and switches. For streaming, LAN quality determines latency, packet loss, and overall image smoothness on the Steam Deck.

Synonyms

home network

Abbreviations
