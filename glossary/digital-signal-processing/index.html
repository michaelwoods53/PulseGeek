Digital Signal Processing

Algorithms that modify audio signals in real time or offline.

In detail

DSP covers filters, dynamics, modulation, and spatial effects used to shape and optimize audio in games. Efficient DSP design balances CPU load with sound quality under runtime constraints.

Synonyms

signal processing, audio DSP

Abbreviations

DSP
