Sarah Bennett

Sarah collects old handhelds and keeps them in careful working order. She loves flea markets, patient repairs, and the satisfying click of a revived cartridge. Storytelling through hardware fascinates her—how form and function evolve together. She enjoys quiet evenings with lo-fi music and a tiny screwdriver set nearby. Preservation feels personal to her, like honoring a shared memory. Friends appreciate her kindness and meticulous care.
