Define terminology once so choices stay consistent. A remap changes button inputs at the RetroArch layer, while a hotkey assigns a meta action like fast-forward or menu toggle. Knowing these constraints helps you prioritize clarity over perfect edge-to-edge fill.

Frequently asked questions

What is the safest hotkey for opening the RetroArch menu on Miyoo?

A reliable pattern is using Select as a modifier with Start as the menu toggle. This combination avoids most in-game inputs yet remains easy to press. Test it in a non-critical title first to confirm it does not overlap with a specific game.

Should I use integer scaling or stretch to fill the screen?

Use integer scaling for sharp pixels. Small borders are normal and can be hidden with a bezel. Stretching to fill often introduces blur and shimmering during scrolls, which is more distracting than modest side or top borders.

When do I create per-game remaps instead of per-core?

Create a per-game remap only when a single title conflicts with your standard layout. Keep per-core remaps for families so most games share a consistent feel. This approach minimizes maintenance and avoids accidental global changes.

Do overlays slow down the Miyoo Mini?

Lightweight static overlays and bezels usually have minimal impact. Complex shader-based effects can harm performance. Start with simple bezel art and avoid heavy shader stacks on the Miyoo Mini to keep input and rendering responsive.

How can I reduce distracting on-screen notifications?

Lower display time for popups and disable nonessential HUD items. Keep only indicators that help diagnose issues. Trimming overlays and notifications frees the viewport and makes latency or stutter easier to evaluate during tests.
