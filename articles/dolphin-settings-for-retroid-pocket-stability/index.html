Frequently asked questions

What is a safe starting resolution for Dolphin on Retroid Pocket?

Start at 1x internal resolution and 4x anisotropic filtering. If frame pacing holds and temperatures remain moderate after a few minutes, try 1.5x. Higher scaling often triggers throttling and stutter on long play sessions.

Should I use Vulkan or OpenGL for most games?

Try Vulkan first because it often delivers smoother frame times on mobile GPUs. If you see UI glitches, shadow issues, or frequent artifacts, switch to OpenGL and retest the same scene for at least two minutes.

When should I disable Dual Core in Dolphin?

Disable Dual Core when you notice timing bugs such as physics jitter, audio desync, or random stutter during cutscenes. Some scripting-heavy titles become more consistent with Dual Core off even though raw performance drops slightly.

How do I reduce audio crackle without big lag?

Increase the audio buffer one step and enable time stretching. Test with a looped scene. If crackle stops but input feels delayed, reduce the buffer one notch while keeping stretching enabled for stability.

Do I need to rebuild shaders after changing backends?

Yes. Switching between Vulkan and OpenGL forces new shader caches. Expect some hitching during the first minutes of play while caches rebuild, which typically settles as you re-visit busy scenes.
