Frequently asked questions

Are 2.4 GHz channels 1, 6, and 11 still the safest default?

Yes, using 1, 6, and 11 avoids overlap in most regions. Keep widths at 20 MHz and limit 2.4 GHz to devices that truly need it to reduce contention with mixed clients.

Should I enable DFS everywhere to increase capacity?

Not by default. Use DFS where surveys show low radar activity and only for traffic that tolerates occasional channel changes. Keep latency sensitive services on stable non-DFS sets.

How wide should channels be in dense deployments?

Start with 20 MHz in high density areas and expand to 40 MHz only where surveys show low contention. Wider channels amplify overlap and can reduce efficiency despite higher peak rates.

When does 6 GHz make the biggest difference?

6 GHz helps most when modern clients dominate and walls contain cells. Use it for heavy data flows while keeping critical voice and control traffic on proven 5 GHz non-DFS lanes.

Do vendor defaults handle clean channel choices well?

Auto settings can work but often need tuning. Validate with surveys, set conservative widths, and override channels when neighbor use or radar hits cause instability.
