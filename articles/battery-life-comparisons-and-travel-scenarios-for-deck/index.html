Frequently asked questions

Is OLED always more efficient than LCD on Deck?

No. OLED can draw less in darker scenes because black pixels are off, but bright scenes narrow or erase the advantage. Keep brightness matched and test dark, bright, and mixed content to see the real spread for your games.

What cap is best for balanced battery and smoothness?

Forty frames with VRR is a reliable starting point for many 3D titles. It often stabilizes frame times while cutting wasteful spikes. If pacing still wobbles, add a modest TDP limit and recheck frame time variance.

Should I limit TDP or use an FPS cap first?

Use an FPS cap first to define the performance floor, then adjust TDP so the game holds that target. This approach preserves responsiveness while removing power bursts that add heat and reduce battery life.

Is it bad to play while charging?

It depends on heat. Charging under heavy load can raise pack temperature. Use moderate charge rates, ventilate the device, and prefer short top‑ups during menus to reduce thermal stress when you must charge while playing.

How should I plan for flights without reliable outlets?

Lower your FPS cap, choose darker UI themes, and disable auto updates. Pack a bank with sufficient output and a short cable. Rotate play and rest intervals to manage heat and conserve battery if outlets are limited.
